## Style guide: the Lantern look
Everything on screen should read as one game with the title and the Journey.

- **Plates.** Panels are `Plate` (`src/ui/plate.gd`): a navy-to-teal gradient (`UiStyle.PLATE_TOP` to `PLATE_BOTTOM`), a 2 px gold line (`UiStyle.BORDER_COLOR`), and for the main panels a fainter inner line with gold corner diamonds (`ornate`). Pop-ups and questions keep `MenuCard`.
- **Colours.** Gold `UiStyle.GOLD_COLOR` for what matters, pale gold `TITLE_COLOR` for headings, `TEXT_COLOR` for text, `DIM_COLOR` for notes, the lantern's teal `LANTERN_COLOR` for light and for Senna, `GOOD_COLOR` and `BAD_COLOR` for gains and losses. No beige, no brown panels.
- **Headings** are spaced capitals in pale gold, with a short gold rule and a diamond where there is room (as the menu card's subtitle has).
- **Cells** (items, skills, spells) are dark wells (`UiStyle.CELL_COLOR`) with a thin rim (`CELL_BORDER`) that turns gold on hover or when chosen.
- **Buttons** use `UiStyle.style_button`.
- **Motion** is short and soft: 0.1–0.2 s fades and slides. Nothing blinks. `GameSettings.low_effects()` turns the extras off.
- **The battlefield** is drawn in code, like the Journey's map: soft shapes, layered tints, a light from the top-left, shadows that ground every wall and unit, and small things that move (motes, ripples, mist). Hitboxes, walls and paths never change.
- **Readability first.** Enemies, red warnings and Lucian must stay the clearest things on screen.

### R1: Resolve
A stage that beats Lucian gets a little easier each time, so every player gets through in the end.
- Each defeat on a story stage **not yet cleared** adds one stack of **Resolve** for that stage, up to 5.
- A stack gives **+10% damage dealt and −10% damage taken** (Normal and Easy). On Hard a stack gives 5%. On Extreme there is no Resolve.
- Clearing the stage clears its stacks. Replays of cleared stages, Training and Endless have none.
- It shows as a buff icon with its stacks, a line on the DEFEATED screen and a line in the Journey's side panel. A Gameplay setting turns it off.
- The save keeps the defeats per stage (`"defeats"`), `VERSION` unchanged.

**As built:** `Resolve` (`src/core/resolve.gd`) holds the rule and its texts; `Difficulty.RESOLVE_STEPS` is `[0.1, 0.1, 0.05, 0.0]`. `Lucian.set_resolve(stacks, fell)` sets `damage_dealt_scale` and `damage_taken_scale` (the difficulty's scale times Resolve's share), read when the stage starts and again when the setting changes. `SaveGame.record_defeat` counts a defeat at once for a story stage not yet cleared, even with the setting off; `finish_stage` erases the stage's entry on a clear. The Gameplay setting is `Resolve.SETTING` (`&"resolve"`, on by default). It shows as:
- a buff icon (`status_empower.svg`) with the stacks as its count and a card, "+30% damage dealt, -30% damage taken. You fell here 3 times.";
- a green line in the DEFEATED screen's rewards, "Resolve 3 / 5: +30% damage, -30% damage taken on your next try";
- a line in the Journey panel's stage box, "Resolve 3 / 5: +30% damage, -30% taken", with a hover text;
- a second line in each difficulty's hover text on the title's difficulty page ("No Resolve: a defeat makes no stage easier." on Extreme).

### B1: the novice bot
The novice tier never moved except out of some warnings, so it measured a player who does not play. It now backs away from melee enemies once it is below ~~half health~~ its tier's `panic` share of health, still reacts in 0.35 s, still misses half the warnings and every bolt.

**As built:** the share is the tier's `panic` (the novice 0.25, so it backs away below a quarter of its health; the other tiers 0), not half. Beyond the plan, the bot grew more tier numbers: `warn` and `bolt` (the share of ground warnings and bolts it dodges), `focus` (it goes for summoners), `fumble` (an action fails), `idle` (seconds it waits in a break before Skip), `between` and `retry` (seconds the play-time estimate adds per stage and per failed try). See "The bot" below.

## As built: the look (L1 to L6)
Every part follows the style guide above. `Plate` and the colour tokens are in `src/ui/plate.gd` and `src/ui/ui_style.gd` (`LANTERN_COLOR`, `PLATE_TOP/BOTTOM`, `CELL_COLOR/CELL_BORDER`, `GOOD_COLOR/BAD_COLOR`, `RIM_FRAGMENT/LEGENDARY/BOOTS/POTION`, `cell_style()`, `item_rim()`). The user-facing description is in `docs/ui_design.md`.

### L1: the HUD
- Plates for the panels (the bottom bar ornate, about 0.92 opaque). A round portrait in a gold ring with the level in a gold badge and a slim gold XP bar.
- `HudBar`: health and mana with a gradient, a tick every 100 HP (a taller one every 1000, none on mana) and a pale ghost of the damage just taken that drains in 0.4 s.
- Cells as dark wells with rims by item kind and keys in corner tabs; rank dots as gold diamonds.
- Stage info: the name in spaced capitals and one diamond pip per wave (`WavePips`: gold cleared, bright current, dim to come, the boss wave's larger; Endless has none).
- An ornate boss bar (red gradient, ghost damage, no ticks; Karthus's break bar under it). Gold corner diamonds on the radar. Buff icons in rounded rimmed cells.
- `LowHealthVignette`: a soft red vignette that pulses below 30% health; off when dead and on low effects.
- `Hud.BOSS_BAR_TOP` is 92 and `Hud.TIPS_TOP` is 180.

### L2: the backpack and the merchant
- The backpack on an ornate plate headed BACKPACK, with dark cells whose rim follows the item and darker locked cells with the chain. The equip and gear rows on plain plates with keys in corner tabs.
- The recipe table names each legendary and lights, in gold and first, every recipe whose two fragments Lucian holds.
- The merchant on an ornate plate headed MERCHANT with the Items, Potions and Craft tabs. Each item for sale is a row (`ShopSlot`: rimmed icon, name, main stat line, price with a coin); SOLD and Locked rows; potion rows with their keys.
- The Craft tab: two inputs, "+", an arrow, the result, a note and the fee. The note, the button and the fee follow the state (empty, one in, no recipe, cannot afford, ready).
- The backpack and the shop fade in over 0.12 s (not on low effects).

### L3: the stage's flow screens
- The end screen: an ornate card with the lantern medallion (warm for STAGE CLEARED, dim red for DEFEATED, GAME OVER and RUN OVER), a large glowing title, stats with icons, and essences that count up over about 0.6 s.
- The Endless modifier pick: CHOOSE A MODIFIER over three ornate cards (icon medallion, downside red, reward green; a lift and a gold rim on hover).
- A wave banner (`WaveBanner`) at each wave's start for about 1.6 s, at `Hud.TIPS_TOP + 70`, never taking input. The tip banner on a plain plate with a gold diamond.

### L4: the maps
`MapArt` (`src/stage/maps/map_art.gd`) and `MapAmbience` (`map_ambience.gd`) are the kit; every map is redrawn with them, and no wall, path, spawn point or size changed. Round and soft shapes are shared textures drawn as rects, and thin shapes are gathered into one mesh, so a map's draw cost is about 0.2 to 1.2 ms of CPU and 0.1 to 0.3 ms of GPU with 40 enemies in view. Ambience is off on low effects. The new `map_art(...)` screenshot scenarios shoot each map whole, at play zoom with units and warnings, and print the cost.
- **L4a:** Act 1's mid lane (cobbles, shaded rocks, crowns, a river with banks and current), Act 4's dark lane (black and violet, wisps, a haze), Act 2's training ground (flagstones, a brass ring with the Sentinels' star, a block wall with braziers, gates with towers, sandstone pillars) and the Training arena (slate slabs, the lantern's teal light).
- **L4b-d:** Act 3's jungle (worn paths, camp details, a dry riverbed, lava scorch in 3-2, clear water in 3-4, fireflies) and the dragon's pit (stone rings, claw marks, an elder glow). Act 5's dead forest (cold grey-teal earth, black leafless tree lines, gnarled trees, Maokai's ring, the spider nest, Elise's camp, Karthus's graveyard with flagstones and tombstones, `MapArt.limbs`). Act 6's Thresh's domain (slate plateaus with lit lips and cliff faces, a layered abyss with far chains and souls, stone bridges with lantern light, the altar, Hecarim's camp) and the final platform (slab courses, 32 glowing runes, a pale dais).
- **L4e: the terrain pieces.** Added after the plan was written. The Black Mist banks are layered mist with a darker heart and wisps, drifting only with `Juice.on()`. Lava (every burning `HazardZone`) is a dark crust with glowing cracks, a hot core and a slow pulse. Shallow water has deeper steps, ripples and glints. The Blast Cone and the Honeyfruit are drawn in code (`MapArt.leaf`); `assets/art/stage/blast_cone.svg` and `honeyfruit.svg` are now unused.

### L5: units and combat
- A soft shadow under every unit. With `Juice.on()` (off on low effects and when run headless): a walk sway, a lunge on a melee hit, a recoil on a shot, a squash when hit, a spawn fade-in and a death burst.
- Enemy health bars with a dark frame, a gradient, a ghost of lost health (holds 0.3 s, then drains) and, for elites, a gold rim and diamond. Lucian's bar is green with ticks every 100 HP.
- `TargetRing`: a thin red ring under the enemy Lucian is ordered to attack.
- `ShotGlow` (a soft glow and trail; not on skin shots), `HitSpark`, damage numbers that pop (crits bigger).
- Ground warnings keep their red fill and gain a dark underline and marching dashes on the edge.

### L6: the main-side screens
- `ScreenFade` (`src/ui/screen_fade.gd`, layer 100): every change of screen, switched by `Game`, fades in from black over 0.25 s. Off on low effects and when run headless.
- The End: `TheEndScreen` shows the title's scene (Thresh hidden, Lucian and Senna) under a dawn tint, with an ornate card on the right (it starts at 36% of the width, so it never covers Lucian's arm).
- The plan also named the slot and difficulty pages and "small consistency fixes"; the change list has nothing for them beyond Resolve's line in the difficulty page's hover texts (R1).

## As built: the rules (R2, R3 and the summon cap)
### R2: the next-wave preview
Before wave 1 and in every break, a plain plate under the stage info (the boss bar's place) shows NEXT and each enemy of the next wave as a 34 px icon with "xN"; a boss or mini boss has a gold rim and its name; hovering names the enemy. Endless only says "Mini boss wave" or "Boss wave". It is hidden in a wave, on the end screen, during the modifier pick and in Training. `WavePreview`, `WaveRunner.next_ids()` and `next_kind()`, `WavePlan.preview_rows()`.

### R3: the death recap
Added after the plan was written. On DEFEATED, GAME OVER and RUN OVER, a WHAT HIT YOU block (`DeathRecap`, `StageStats.record_hit`): "Killed by <name>" ("Killed by burning ground", "Killed by Gromp's burn"), the top three damage sources as shares, one tip chosen by the top source (boss, siege marks, elite, ranged, melee, burning ground), and "You can afford an upgrade in the Evolution Tree." when the save can buy a node. Nothing shows when Lucian took no damage.

### The summon cap
Added after the plan was written. `EnemyData.summon_total`: a Maiden of the Mist calls 12 ghouls and a Broodmother 10 spiderlings in all, then no more. It was no limit before; only the cap on live summons held them. A call is cut to what is left of the total.

## As built: the tools and the bot
- `Stage.confine_mouse` (true in the game), `GameSettings.owns_window` (true) and `Pointer` (`src/core/pointer.gd`: the game reads the mouse through it; `Pointer.pretend` places a pretend mouse). With `override.cfg` (git-ignored) the screenshot tool runs off-screen: `SCREENSHOT_OFFSCREEN=1` moves its window to (20000, 20000) at 1600x900, `SCREENSHOT_RUN=<name>` gives a run its own save and settings files, it stops if its window moved and it never warps the real mouse.
- **The bot** (`tests/tools/autoplay.gd`): `AUTOPLAY_RUN`, `AUTOPLAY_TIER="panic=0.2,..."` and `AUTOPLAY_RESOLVE=off`. It walks to the merchant, waits its tier's `idle` seconds before Skip, goes for summoners (`focus`), fumbles (`fumble`) and backs away when hurt (`panic`). Each try prints its Resolve. A run ends with a per-stage summary and an estimated play time: the stage time, plus `between` seconds per stage or farm run, plus `retry` seconds per failed try.

| Tier | react | kite | panic | warn | bolt | focus | fumble | idle | between | retry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skilled | 0.15 | 90 | 0 | 1.0 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 0 | 1 | 45 | 10 |
| Average | 0.25 | 40 | 0 | 0.8 | 0.2 | 0.5 | 0.08 | 3 | 75 | 15 |
| Novice | 0.35 | never | 0.25 | 0.5 | 0 | 0 | 0.25 | 6 | 120 | 20 |
