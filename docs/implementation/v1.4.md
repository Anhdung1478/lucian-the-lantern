# V1.4: Boots System and potion cells — Implementation Plan

> **Project:** `D:\Project\lucian-the-lantern` (Godot 4.7.2 game).
> **Status:** planned and approved on 2026-11-06; built the same day (report: `docs/report/v1.4.md`). The tier-3 boots keep their tier-2 effect, as assumed below.

## Context
V1.4 adds two features:
1. **Two free potion cells** (Health and Mana) on keys 7 and 8. Potions can no longer go in equip slots 1–6.
2. **A 16-boot system.**
   - Tier-1 boots are bought once on the Evolution Tree and kept forever. Boots of Speed costs blue essence; Slightly Magical Boots is its blue + orange upgrade.
   - Lucian starts **every map** with them in a new boots cell: story stages, the tutorial, Training and Endless.
   - Boots are upgraded at a new **Boots** tab in the merchant: the player puts in the boots and a fragment of the right type and pays a gold fee.
   - Boots can't be sold, bought or dropped. Stats and passives follow LoL.

This is version **1.4.0**.

## Design decisions
**Potion cells**
- Each cell holds at most 9, like a backpack cell.
- Bought potions fill the matching potion cell first, then the backpack.
- Potions can only be used from the two potion cells (keys 7 and 8) or the backpack's Use button; they can't go in equip slots 1–6.
- The player refills the cells by hand; there is no auto-refill.
- An empty cell shows its potion's icon, faded.

**Boots**
- Boots go in their own boots cell. It has no key, because boots have only stats and passives.
- Boots of Speed (blue) and Slightly Magical Boots (blue + orange) are two levels of one Evolution Tree node, bought once and kept forever.
- Slightly Magical gives +10 move speed over Boots of Speed, and every boot upgraded from it keeps the +10. Which one was bought is read from the tree level, so there is no new save data.
- Upgrades happen only at the merchant's Boots tab: the player puts in the boots and a fragment of the right type and pays a gold fee. The fee is a per-boot LoL combine cost. Players can't upgrade by crafting in the backpack.
- Boots can't be sold. The merchant never sells boots and enemies never drop them.
- Stats and passives follow LoL.
- Lucian starts every map with the boots he has bought.
- Giant's Belt (HP) has no boots line.

## Boot data (LoL 26.x values; each lives in a `.tres` so it can be tuned)
**Tier 1** (from the Evolution Tree, no fee):
- Boots of Speed: +25 MS.
- Slightly Magical Boots: +25 MS; *Slightly Quicker:* +10 MS, kept by every upgrade.

Each tier-2 boot is made from either tier-1 boot plus the line's fragment. Each tier-3 boot is made from its tier-2 boot plus the same fragment. The fee is in brackets.

| Line | Tier 2 (fee) | Tier-2 stats / passive | Tier 3 (fee) | Tier-3 stats / passive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS (Recurve Bow) | Berserker's Greaves (300) | +30% AS, +45 MS | Gunmetal Greaves (500) | +45% AS, +5% life steal, +45 MS |
| CR (Cloak of Agility) | Boots of Swiftness (700) | +55 MS, +25% slow resist | Swiftmarch (500) | +65 MS, +25% slow resist; *Noxian Fervor:* adaptive force = 5% of move speed |
| AD (B.F. Sword) | Gluttonous Greaves (800) | +45 MS, +4% omnivamp; *Slay* | Immortal Path (500) | the same plus *Now and Forever* |
| MP (Tear) | Ionian Boots of Lucidity (350) | +10 AH, +45 MS, +10 summoner haste | Crimson Lucidity (500) | +20 AH, +45 MS, +20 summoner haste; *Noxian Haste* |
| MR (Negatron Cloak) | Mercury's Treads (550) | +20 MR, +45 MS, +30% tenacity | Chainlaced Crushers (500) | +25 MR, +45 MS, +30% tenacity\*; *Noxian Persistence* |
| AR (Chain Vest) | Plated Steelcaps (600) | +25 AR, +45 MS; *Plating* | Armored Advance (500) | +35 AR, +45 MS, *Plating*\*; *Noxian Endurance* |
| AP (Rod) | Sorcerer's Shoes (800) | +12 flat magic pen, +45 MS | Spellslinger's Shoes (500) | +20 flat magic pen, +8% magic pen, +45 MS |

\* LoL's own pages don't confirm whether these tier-3 boots keep the tier-2 effect. I assume they do; it is easy to remove.

**The passives in this game:**
- **Slay:** +0.6% omnivamp for each elite, mini boss or boss kill, up to 10 stacks. Stacks last for the stage and carry over to Immortal Path.
- **Now and Forever:** at 50% HP or more, Lucian deals 4% more damage. Below 50%, healing, shields and regen are 12% stronger.
- **Noxian Fervor:** adaptive force uses AD by default (0.6 AD per point), or AP when bonus AP is higher.
- **Noxian Haste:** a skill hit or a summoner spell gives +8% move speed for 4 s. It can trigger once every 4 s.
- **Noxian Persistence / Endurance:** taking magic damage (Persistence) or physical damage (Endurance) gives a shield against that damage type of 100–200 by level (+8% bonus HP) for 5 s. 15 s cooldown.
- **Plating:** enemy basic attacks deal 10% less damage. Burn ticks don't count.
- "Champion" triggers count any enemy.

**Boots tree node:** level 1 (Boots of Speed) costs 300 blue; level 2 (Slightly Magical Boots) costs 800 blue + 10 orange. Both are tunable placeholders, and the node must never cost 0, because the FREE-badge tests depend on it.

## Implementation steps

### 1. Inventory cells (`src/core/inventory.gd`)
- **New constants:**
  - `EQUIP_END := 60`
  - `HP_POTION_CELL := 60`, `MP_POTION_CELL := 61`, `BOOTS_CELL := 62`
  - `SIZE := 63`
- **Switch equip loops to `EQUIP_END`:** `is_equip`, `is_locked`, the `unlocked_equip` setter and `equipped()` use `EQUIP_END` instead of `SIZE`. Potion cells and the boots cell are never locked.
- **New `accepts(index, item)`:**
  - backpack: anything but boots;
  - equip: anything but potions and boots;
  - HP cell: potions with `restore_hp > 0`;
  - MP cell: potions with `restore_mana > 0`;
  - boots cell: nothing by drag.
- **Drop rules:** `can_put` and `drop_outcome` check `accepts` both ways, for the item moving in and, on a swap, for the item coming back. A drag from or onto `BOOTS_CELL` always returns `RETURN`: boots never leave.
- **Potions:** `add()` and `space_for()` fill the matching potion cell first (up to `max_stack` 9), then the backpack.
- **Boots:** `equipped()` includes the boots cell, so its stats and passives count. New `boots()` / `set_boots(item)`. `clear()` keeps the boots.
- **New `key_action(index)`:** returns `item_N` for equip slots, `potion_hp` / `potion_mp` for the potion cells, and `&""` otherwise.

### 2. Item data and catalog
- **`src/core/item_data.gd`:**
  - `Kind.BOOTS`
  - exports `boots_tier`, `upgrade_from` (the tier-2 id a tier-3 boot is upgraded from), `upgrade_fragment` and `upgrade_cost`
  - `is_boots()`
  - `sell_price()` returns 0 for boots
- **`src/core/item_catalog.gd`:** `boots()` and `boots_upgrade(boots, fragment) -> ItemData`. Tier 1 + a line's fragment gives that line's tier 2; tier 2 + its own fragment gives its tier 3; anything else gives null. `combine()` stays fragment-only.
- **Data files:**
  - 16 files in `data/items/boots/*.tres`, added to `data/items/catalog.tres`
  - 16 icons in `assets/art/items/<id>.svg`, hand-made SVGs in the same style as the existing ones
- **`src/core/item_stats.gd`:** new keys `slow_resist` (%), `summoner_haste` and `magic_pen_flat`. Each needs:
  - a `StatBlock` property
  - an entry in the pass-through list in `unit_stats.gd` L58
  - an entry in `StatStyle` (KEYS/ICONS/COLORS/DESCRIPTIONS, or alias `magic_pen_flat` → the `magic_pen` icon in `_look()`)
  - new icons in `assets/art/stats/`
  - entries in `StatsPanel.EXTRA`, which grows from 8 to 11 cells

### 3. Boots out of the merchant, drops and sell
- `MerchantStock._roll` and `LootTable.roll` already only use `legendaries()` and `fragments()`, so they stay as they are. A test will check that.
- `Shop.sell` / `sell_value` refuse boots.
- In `backpack_screen._release`, dropping boots on SELL returns them with the message "Boots can't be sold."

### 4. Evolution Tree node
- **`data/tree/boots.tres`:** branch backpack, grid (2, 1.5) on the trunk between the two plaques (checked to fit with 20 px gaps), max_level 2, costs as above. Add it to `data/tree/catalog.tres`.
- **Icon:** `assets/art/tree/boots.svg`.
- **`TreeNodeData`:** add `level_texts: Array[String]`. `value_text()` uses it, so the node reads "None → Boots of Speed → Slightly Magical Boots" with no UI change.
- **`tree_page.gd`:** add `BOOTS_ID` and a `_draw_boots_preview()` plaque (two boot slots, lit per level) that reuses `_draw_plaque` and `_draw_slot`. Update the Backpack BLURB. Add the id to `tree_art.gd` `_blocked()`.

### 5. Stage setup and Lucian
- **`StageSetup`:**
  - `boots_level` is read from `tree.level(&"boots")` in `from_save()`.
  - `for_stage()` (tutorial included) and `for_endless()` build on `from_save()` and keep it, so every map starts with the boots bought.
  - The sandbox default is 2.
- **`Lucian.apply_setup`** (lucian.gd ~435):
  - puts the boots item (`boots_of_speed` or `slightly_magical_boots`) in `BOOTS_CELL`;
  - stores `boots_level`.
- **`_stat_bonus()`:** adds +10 flat move speed when `boots_level == 2` and the boots cell is not empty. This is Slightly Quicker, and it carries through upgrades without extra save data. It also shows as a buff-bar entry.

### 6. Passives and new rules (`src/lucian/items/item_passives.gd` and its hooks)
- **Slow resist:** `Lucian.slow_amount()` multiplies by `1 - stats.slow_resist`. The "Slowed" buff text does the same.
- **Summoner haste:** `SummonerSpells.cooldown_of()` wraps its value in `Cooldown.hasted(…, lucian.stats.summoner_haste)`. Update the comments that say haste doesn't apply.
- **Flat magic pen:** `DamageCalc._penetrate` becomes `maxf(resist*(1-pen) - flat, 0)` when resist > 0. Carry it on `DamageInfo` from `make_damage`, and pass it from both `mitigate` callers (enemy.gd L75, lucian.gd L635).
- **Plating:** a new `passives.damage_taken_multiplier(info)` is applied in `Lucian.take_damage` before `mitigate`. It is ×0.9 when `info.source == &"basic" and not info.over_time`.
- **Slay:** `Lucian.on_kill` passes the enemy to `passives.on_kill(enemy)`. Stacks count when `enemy.data.kind` is elite, mini_boss or boss. `adjust_stats` adds the omnivamp, and the buff icon shows the count. The stacks aren't reset when Gluttonous Greaves becomes Immortal Path: the reset in `refresh()` only happens when neither boot is equipped.
- **Now and Forever:**
  - Damage: `before_damage` multiplies by 1.04 at 50% HP or more.
  - Healing: `Lucian.heal()`, `add_shield()` and the regen tick read a new `passives.heal_scale()` (1.12 below 50% HP) on top of `heal_shield_power`.
- **Noxian Fervor:** in `adjust_stats`, after the other passives, from `stats.move_speed`.
- **Noxian Haste:** in `on_damage_dealt` (skill sources) plus a new `on_summoner_used()` called from `SummonerSpells.use()`. It calls `lucian.add_speed_buff(&"noxian_haste", 0, 0.08, 4.0)` behind a 4 s internal cooldown.
- **Persistence / Endurance:** in `on_damage_taken` by damage type. `Shields` gets a `physical_only` flag next to `magic_only`, and the ids `persistence` and `endurance` are added to `ORDER` after `magebane`. Their cooldowns appear in `buffs()`.

### 7. Merchant Boots tab (`src/ui/shop/shop_panel.gd`, `src/core/shop.gd`, `backpack_screen.gd`)
- **`Shop.upgrade_boots(boots_index, fragment_index) -> Result`:**
  - checks `catalog.boots_upgrade` and the gold;
  - spends `result.upgrade_cost`;
  - removes the fragment;
  - calls `inventory.set_boots(result)`.
- **New `Shop.Result` values:** `NOT_UPGRADABLE`, `NO_BOOTS` and `WRONG_CELL` (for example, a potion bought straight into an equip slot). Each gets a message in `MESSAGES`.
- **Shop panel:**
  - Tabs become `["Items", "Potions", "Boots"]`. `slots(tab)` returns only ShopSlot children. Refresh shows on tab 0 only.
  - The hint text depends on the tab.
- **Boots page:** `[boots input] + [fragment input] → [result preview]`, the fee (coin + gold), an **Upgrade** button and a message line. Inputs are a new small `UpgradeSlot` control.
  - They hold an inventory index, not the item, so nothing has to be returned when the shop closes.
  - They are cleared on close and re-checked on `inventory.changed`.
  - The page says "No boots: buy them in the Evolution Tree" when the boots cell is empty.
- **Drag targets in `backpack_screen.gd`:**
  - New constants `UPGRADE_BOOTS := -3` and `UPGRADE_FRAGMENT := -4`. `_cell_index_at` and `target_index` return them over the inputs. `_release` loads the dragged cell into that input, refusing the wrong kind.
  - Dragging from the boots cell is allowed (it can only land on the boots input).
  - Build cells 60–62 before the sell cell, so `_cells[i].index == i` and the sell slot stay last.

### 8. Backpack screen, cells and HUD
- **`item_cell.gd`:**
  - new `Style.POTION` and `Style.BOOTS`, with FILLS/BORDERS entries;
  - an empty potion cell draws its potion icon at about 30% alpha; the empty boots cell does the same with the Boots of Speed icon;
  - key labels come from `inventory.key_action(index)`;
  - empty boots cell tooltip: "Buy Boots in the Evolution Tree".
- **`backpack_screen._build`:** a new row under the equip strip: `[Boots] … [HP 7][MP 8]`. A right-click on a potion cell still shows the Use button.
- **HUD (`hud.gd`, `hud.tscn`):** a new `%GearColumn` beside `%ItemGrid` (not inside it, because tests count `item_grid` children). It holds:
  - two `HudItemSlot`s for cells 60 and 61, with counts and key labels;
  - the boots icon, with no key, hidden when empty.

  `HudItemSlot` takes a cell index instead of the equip slot number; update its one caller.
- **Tooltips (`tooltip_card.gd`):**
  - `KIND_NAMES[BOOTS] = "Boots"`;
  - the header shows "Can't be sold" instead of a gold price for boots;
  - the boots footer gives the upgrade path, for example "Upgrade at the merchant with a Recurve Bow (500 gold)", or "Fully upgraded";
  - the potion footer becomes "…put it in its potion cell and press 7 / 8."
- **`item_icon.gd`:** a fallback colour for BOOTS.

### 9. Keys and settings
- **`project.godot`:** actions `potion_hp` (physical keycode 55) and `potion_mp` (56).
- **`game_settings.gd`:** add them to `REBINDABLE` and `ACTION_NAMES` ("Health potion", "Mana potion"). They are **not** `CASTABLE`, so they get no cast mode.
- **`settings_screen.gd`:** add them to the ITEMS group in `CONTROL_GROUPS`.
- **`lucian_controller._unhandled_input`:** a branch for the two actions that calls a new `Lucian.use_potion_cell(index)`. It has the same `can_act()` guard as `use_equip_slot`, then calls `use_item`. There is no auto-refill.
