Their commits were then cherry-picked onto `v1.4.3` in the user's order. The notes marked **As built** below record where the build differs from this plan.

  - Messages: `Fix B1: <what now works>` / `Change 1: <...>`. Each is GPG-signed (the default) and ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` and the `Claude-Session:` line. Nothing is pushed.
- **Version:** 1.4.3.
- **Tests:** each step adds or updates GUT tests. Tests that pin the old behaviour are rewritten, not deleted.

## Steps

### B1: Death's Dance's delayed damage survives an unequip
`ItemPassives.refresh()` (`src/lucian/items/item_passives.gd:190`) no longer clears `_bleeds` when Death's Dance is gone. A bleed already owed runs out on its own; only Defy (the takedown cleanse) and Guardian Angel's revive clear it, as now. The Ignore Pain buff icon (Fix 37) keeps showing while damage is owed, with or without the item.
- **Tests:** unequip and re-equip mid-bleed: the full delayed damage still lands. Sell + Undo sale: same.

### B3: Elise doesn't start Rappel while her Cocoon web flies
`elise.gd` `_can_use` (93) refuses Rappel while one of her Cocoon webs is in flight (as well as while Lucian is stunned, Fix 36). She does something else and keeps Rappel's cooldown.
- **Tests:** web in flight → no Rappel; web gone → Rappel allowed.

### B4: Quitting during a boss cutscene keeps the clear
`Game._save()` (`src/main/game.gd:148`): if a stage cutscene is running, skip it first so the clear and its rewards run, then save the way the result panel does. Act 1's and 6-5's cutscenes both.
- **Tests:** a 6-5 clear mid-Senna-scene + quit-save → 6-5 recorded, Thresh's reward paid, `ending_seen` handling unchanged.

### B2: Buff and stat cards stay live
`BuffIcon` (`src/ui/hud/buff_bar.gd`) and `StatCell` (`src/ui/hud/stat_cell.gd`) get `tooltip_version()`, like the skill slots (Fix 18): for a buff, its id, count, seconds left (to 0.1 s) and text; for a stat cell, its shown value text. `HoverTip` rebuilds the open card when it changes, so a reused icon never describes the old buff.
- **Tests:** card text follows a buff that moves into the hovered icon; "X s left" counts down while hovered.

### B5: Guardian Angel's stasis stops only buff timers
During stasis, **skill, summoner and item cooldowns all count down** (item cooldowns now too). **Only buff timers stop** (including Barrier's shield time). `lucian.gd:1319`: tick `item_cooldowns` before the stasis `return`.
- **Tests:** an item on cooldown loses 4 s during a 4 s stasis; a buff keeps its time.
- **As built:** a follow-up commit also stops the buff timers that `ItemPassives` keeps itself: Spear of Shojin's Focused Will and Death's Dance's bleed. Item waits that act like cooldowns keep running: Kaenic Rookern's Magebane wait, Malignance's Hatefog cooldown, Rapid Firecannon's wait, and the combat timer behind Jak'Sho. The attack timer counts down too. HP and mana regen stay stopped, as before.

### B6: Lightslinger never throws away the second shot
`lightslinger.gd:35`: arming a new second shot while one is still pending fires the pending one at once first (its damage and its E cooldown cut), then arms the new one.
- **Tests:** two charged attacks inside 0.15 s → two second shots land and E is cut twice.

### B7: Cards keep the short key names (doc change)
**The user's call:** short names stay on the cards too, because a long name such as "Backspace" can overflow a skill's box. `docs/ui_design.md` (and any doc or comment that says cards keep full names) now says HUD slots **and their hover cards** use `short_key_label`; Settings and sentence-style hints keep full names. Code: make skill and summoner cards consistent (both short). Update any test that pins full names on the cards.

### B8: Craft inputs hold the item, and Craft asks first
**The user's design:**
- Dragging a backpack item into a Craft input **takes it out of the backpack view**: its cell shows empty and can't be dragged, sold, used or filled while the item sits in the input. (The inventory model keeps it in its cell, so nothing is lost on a save or a crash; the cell counts as taken.)
- It **comes back** when it's dragged from the input back to the backpack (onto its own cell, or onto another free cell that accepts it, which moves it there), when the merchant tab changes, or when the merchant closes. A right-click on the input still empties it (it comes back).
- One cell can't be in both inputs: it isn't draggable once loaded. Dragging from one input to the other moves it.
- **Craft asks first:** pressing Craft opens a confirm pop-up naming the result, the two inputs and the fee (e.g. "Craft Muramana from Tear of the Goddess and Tear of the Goddess for 2100 gold?"). Yes crafts; No leaves everything as it was.
- **Tests:** loaded cell is hidden and refuses drops; tab change / close returns it; can't load one cell twice; drag-back moves to the drop cell; Craft → pop-up → Yes crafts, No doesn't.
- **As built:**
  - A follow-up commit makes a craft empty both inputs, so upgraded boots show in their cell at once, as the legendary does.
  - A fee you can't afford is refused with "Not enough gold" without asking.
  - Dropping an input's item onto a full input swaps the two.
  - Only a free cell counts as a place to drop an input's item; anywhere else, it goes back to its own cell.

### B9: A locked merchant slot explains itself
Right-clicking a locked (chained) merchant slot gives a locked result ("Locked", not "Already sold"), and hovering it shows a note naming the Evolution Tree node that opens it, as a locked backpack cell does.
- **Tests:** right-click locked slot → locked message; hover → note with the node's name.

### B10: Blast Cone shows its reach and the landing spot
**The user's call:** being thrown into 3-2's lava is a feature, not a bug. Instead the Blast Cone shows:
- **Its reach** (`REACH`, 350 px): a faint ring on the ground while it's ripe, a little brighter while Lucian stands inside it.
- **The landing spot** while Lucian is inside the reach and a throw would happen (`landing_spot` returns somewhere else): a marker at that spot, recomputed every frame so it moves as he moves. Nothing when no throw would happen or the cone is popped.
- Colors from the cone's own palette (its soft yellow `GLOW`), drawn under the units, never red (red means enemy danger).
- **Tests:** marker shown/hidden by distance and ripeness; marker position equals `landing_spot` and follows Lucian.

### B11: Quick cast with indicator keeps the aim through CC
`lucian_controller.gd:125`: releasing a held skill key while Lucian can't act keeps the aim (as normal cast mode does since Fix 29) and fires it once he can act, unless it's cancelled or he dies.
- **Tests:** hold Q, stun, release → Q fires when the stun ends.

### B12: With Cast queue off, nothing waits
`lucian.gd:578`/`296`: `_walk_into_reach` for Gunblade or Smite is only set when Lucian isn't cast-locked or dashing; otherwise, with the queue off, the press is refused like the in-reach case, and the attack order is kept.
- **Tests:** queue off, Q cast time, out-of-reach Gunblade/Smite → refused, attack order unchanged.

### B13: Burns from normal and elite enemies scale
`enemy_unit.gd`: `hit_burn` is scaled by `damage / data.damage` (equals `skill_scale` for an Endless boss, so bosses don't double-scale; check and keep Fix B's result). Story enemies keep their burn.
- **Tests:** an Endless-scaled Gromp's burn scales; a story Gromp's burn is unchanged; a boss isn't scaled twice.
- **As built:** story enemies' ratio isn't 1. Their hits grow with the act and wave (an Act 3 Gromp hits 1.3× at wave 1), so applying the ratio everywhere would have raised Act 3's story burns. To keep story burns unchanged, the ratio is set only by the Endless director, as a new `EnemyUnit.burn_scale` (default 1.0) that replaces `skill_scale` in `apply_on_hit`.

### B14: Cone warnings count Lucian's body at the sides
`ground_warning.gd` `in_cone` (157): add an angular margin `asin(min(1, point_radius / distance))` so a body overlapping the cone's side edge is hit, like circles, lines and rings.
- **Tests:** a point just outside the side edge but within its radius is in; farther out is not.

### B15: Hidden screens stop animating
- The Journey only processes while `is_visible_in_tree()` (another section shown → it stops), as `TreePage` does.
- While **Settings** is open over the main screen, the Tree, the Skin Shop and the Journey stop redrawing and processing; they resume when Settings closes.
- **Tests:** Journey stops processing when another section shows; all three stop while Settings is open and resume after.
- **As built:** Settings' backdrop is see-through (black at 55%), so hiding the sections would have changed the look. Instead, the section area's `process_mode` is disabled while Settings is open, through Fix E's `_update_covered()`.

### B16: The backpack recentres when the window changes
`backpack_screen.gd:86`: `_place_column()` runs on the viewport's `size_changed` (connected once, disconnected on exit).
- **Tests:** resize the viewport while the backpack is set up → column centred.

### B17: Four stale texts
- `data/tree/boots.tres`: the Boots node points to the merchant's **Craft** tab.
- `shop_panel.gd:22`: the Potions tab hint is built from the potion actions' `key_label`, not "cells 7 and 8".
- `docs/lucian_game_design.md:979`: `endless_map` 0 is Random, 1–6 the maps (6 is Thresh's domain).
- `docs/items.md:22`: a crafted legendary sells for less than its fragments **plus the craft fee**.

### B18: Debug tools leave nothing broken
- Dark Lucian gets an `_exit_tree()` that finishes his dash trail, so a debug wave jump mid-dash leaves no violet trail.
- `thresh_1` (and any boss that needs the stage's phase flow to end) is dropped from the debug spawn list, or a debug-spawned one is wired so its wave can end — the implementer picks the simpler correct one and says which.
- **Tests:** freeing Dark Lucian mid-dash frees the trail; the debug list has no phase boss (or the spawned one ends).
- **As built:** `thresh_1` stays in the list and is wired. `Stage.spawn_unit` connects `phase_ended` for every plain Thresh. The boss wave's Thresh plays the cutscene as before. Any other Thresh leaves at 50% with his blue and orange, counted out of his wave, and the fight goes on.

### B19: Settings survive a failed rename
`game_settings.gd`: on a failed rename the `.tmp` is kept (not deleted), and `load` falls back to `settings.json.tmp` when `settings.json` is missing or unreadable. Sliders in Settings save on `drag_ended` (and on a keyboard/wheel change) rather than on every step.
- **Tests:** only the `.tmp` exists → its settings load; a slider drag saves once.

### Change 1: Difficulty leaves the gear menu
The gear menu's greyed Difficulty info row is removed. Difficulty is chosen only on the title's difficulty page after "New game" and is never shown or changed in a game. Remove `DIFFICULTY_ICON` and the row's code if unused; update `MenuKeys` wiring and the docs (`main_screen.md`, `ui_design.md`, design doc).
- **Tests:** the gear menu has no Difficulty row.

### Change 2: Lucian's Journey token follows the selected stage
The token (skin portrait) floats over **the selected node** (`JourneyMap.select`) and moves when the selection changes; the latest open stage gets no token of its own. When the Journey opens, the selection is still the latest open stage, so the token starts there.
- **Tests:** select a cleared stage → token over it; select another → it moves.
- **As built:** the token follows any selected place, Training and the Endless portal included. `token_node` is renamed `next_node`. With every stage cleared, the Journey opens on Training and the token sits there; before, it disappeared.
