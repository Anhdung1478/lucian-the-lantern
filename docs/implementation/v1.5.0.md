> **Project:** `E:\Project\lucian-the-latern` (Godot 4.7.2 game).
> **Status:** decided by the user on 2026-11-11 (answers below); built the same day (report: `docs/report/v1.5.0.md`).

## Context
V1.4.3 fixed the review's bugs. This version takes the review's balance findings, three of the four items still open from V1.3.0 (20 is skipped), and the synthesized score (N25). The user also asked to narrow the fragment prices, so legendary craft fees even out (P7). P2 is not a separate item: P1, P4 and P5 soften the walls it describes.

- **Branch:** `v1.5.0` off `v1.4.3` (HEAD `b1f988f`).
- **How it was built:** six agents worked in parallel, each in its own worktree and branch (items and fragments; progression and rewards; Endless; items and skins; debug, Journey and healers; music). Their commits were cherry-picked onto `v1.5.0` in this order with no conflicts. **As built** notes below record where the build differs from this plan.
- **Commits:** one per item, in this order: 16, 19, 31, N25, P1, P3, P4, P5, P6, P8, P11, P9, P10, P12, P13, P14, P15, P16, P17, P18, P7 (the fragments). Then a version/docs commit. Messages: `Fix 16: …`, `N25: …`, `P1: …`; signed, with the Co-Authored-By and Claude-Session trailers. Nothing is pushed.
- **Version:** 1.5.0.
- **Tests:** each step adds or updates GUT tests. Tests that pin old numbers are updated, not deleted.
- **Bots:** after the merge, the skilled and the average bot run the story before (V1.4.3) and after, 25 tries per stage, to measure the balance pass against the 5–7 h target.

## The user's answers
- **31:** the Journey always opens on the **latest open stage** (the first open one not cleared), or Training when every stage is cleared.
- **P15:** the penetration items are **Lord Dominik's Regards, Mortal Reminder and Void Staff**. Only **one** of them can be equipped at a time (so two of them, of any kind, are blocked). Boots don't count. **Shadowflame becomes 15 flat magic penetration** (`magic_pen_flat`), outside the rule.
- **P15:** with a full backpack, a shop item can still be **dragged onto a free equip slot** (an explicit choice), if the pen rule allows.
- **P5:** R's rank 1 becomes a free claim after Act 2 but **keeps** its other requirements (the passive learned, and Q, W and E with 5 ranks between them).
- **P7 and the fragments:** fragments go from 400–1,300 to 650–1,100, with stats scaled to the new cost. **The fee stays legendary − both fragments**, which now runs about 900–1,600 (was 800–2,100).
- **P11:** the wave 10/20/30/40 payouts are paid **the first time a save reaches them**.
- **P17:** Black Mist regeneration (Act 5), the Maiden's drain (Act 5) and Thresh harvesting souls (Act 6).

## Steps

### 16: Shieldbow counts only shields that would absorb the hit
`item_passives.gd` (~352): Lifeline's "shield already up" check uses only the shields that would absorb this hit's damage type. Magebane and Persistence are magic only, and Armored Advance's Endurance is physical only. Add a `ShieldPool` helper if one is missing, e.g. `total_for(type)`.

### 19: The debug tables open only in debug builds
F3 + Q opens the stage debug panel (`src/ui/debug/debug_panel.gd:21`) and the main screen's debug table (`src/ui/debug/main_debug_table.gd:52`) only when `OS.is_debug_build()`. Tests run in a debug build, so they keep working; add a seam so a test can check the release case.

### 31: The Journey opens on the latest open stage
`journey_section.gd`: every time the Journey opens or refreshes its default pick, including after a story stage, after Training and after an Endless run, it selects the first open stage not cleared (`map.next_node`), else Training. The Endless map choice is still remembered (Fix 32). The test that pins 1-1 after the tutorial is rewritten (it now opens on 2-1: Act 1 has only one stage).
- **As built:** Act 1 has one stage, so after the tutorial the Journey opens on **2-1**. Switching main-screen tabs keeps the player's own pick. `JourneySection.open_on_next()` replaces the per-act rules, and `Game.show_main_screen()` takes no arguments.

### N25: A synthesized, adaptive score
All music is made in code, as `sfx.gd` makes the sound effects. There are no audio files.
- A `Music` node, an autoload or owned by `Game`, on the existing **Music** bus. The Settings slider stops saying "No music yet".
- **Pieces:**
  - one per act: its own mode or scale, tempo and drone;
  - a calm theme for the title and the main screen.
- **Layers** of one piece, the same length and looped in sync, faded in and out separately:
  - the drone or pad (always on);
  - a pulse or percussion layer, louder with more enemies alive;
  - a two-voice motif for Lucian and Senna. Senna's voice drops out after Act 1 and comes back once 6-5 has been cleared (she is freed);
  - a boss layer, on the boss or mini boss wave;
  - a quiet break layer: the pulse drops out during breaks.
- **Rendering:** loops are rendered at startup or lazily to `AudioStreamWAV` (mono, 22,050 Hz or similar, a few bars), cached per piece. Crossfade between pieces.
- **Hooks:** the stage's wave, boss and break signals, and the alive-enemy count from `WaveRunner`.
- **Costs:** keep CPU and startup low, and keep the test suite fast. Rendering must be cheap or skippable in tests, with a test seam.
- **Tests:** layer volumes follow the state, the act picks its piece, the Senna rule, and the Settings label.
- **As built:** `src/audio/score.gd` (the synthesizer) and `src/audio/music.gd` (the player), a node owned by Game, no autoload. Seven pieces, each with a mode, tempo and drone. Five layers in one `AudioStreamSynchronized`. Rendered once per piece on worker threads (about 0.2–0.7 s), at most three pieces kept (about 10 MB). It reads the Stage and WaveRunner state each frame instead of hooking signals. Nothing renders headless. Paused: −6 dB.

A `GOLD_SHARES` table next to `HP_SHARES` (`src/core/enemy_data.gd:10`) scales kill gold by act or kind, so the acts' gold per stage climbs smoothly. Today Act 3 averages 3.9k, Act 4 10.6k and Act 5 7.7k.
- Act 3 comes up toward the line between Acts 2 and 4.
- 3-1's first break reaches the cheapest fragment, **650 after P7** (Tear).
- Endless keeps its pay (P11 handles it).
- Report the per-act gold before and after.
- **As built:** The table lives next to `HP_SHARES` in `src/core/wave_plan.gd`. Act 3's normals and elites get ×1.7 gold in 3-1 to 3-5: Act 3 averages 6,435 a stage (was 3,859; the Act 2–4 line is 6,789), and 3-1's first break is about 675 (was 397). Act 5 is left as it is: P1's Maokai cut already covers 5-2.

### P4: X-2's normal enemies have 0.9 HP
In every act's stage 2 (2-2 … 6-2; Act 1 has only one stage), normal enemies spawn with ×0.9 HP. Elites, mini bosses and bosses are unchanged. No extra start level.
- **As built:** Act 1 has no second stage, so the share applies to 2-2 … 6-2. Maiden ghouls aren't listed, as in 5-1; summoned spiderlings get it.

### P5: Cheaper skill ranks 2–3, and a free R rank 1 after Act 2
- **Q, W and E:** rank 2 costs 200 → 150 blue, rank 3 costs 350 → 250 blue.
- **R rank 1:** costs 0, and opens only once Act 2 is cleared, so it shows as a free claim like E's rank 1. It keeps the passive and the Q+W+E 5-rank requirement. Ranks 2–3 are unchanged.
- An old save that already bought R rank 1 keeps it.
- **As built:** R uses a per-level act gate (`level_acts = [2, 0, 0]`), so an old save that owns rank 1 can still buy rank 2. A tree reset refunds at today's prices, so a rank 1 bought for 600 blue + 10 orange refunds nothing.

### P6: Orange drop chance grows with the act
Normal and elite orange chance × (1 + 0.2 × (act − 1)): ×1.0 in Act 1 up to ×2.0 in Act 6. Endless uses its reward act.

### P8: Hard and Extreme pay more orange too
The difficulty's essence scale (Hard ×1.2, Extreme ×1.5) multiplies orange as it does blue: the drop chance, and the sure orange of bosses and mini bosses (rounded). Rename `BLUE_SCALES` to `ESSENCE_SCALES`. The difficulty text says "Blue and orange essence ×1.2".
- **As built:** The Rift Scuttler's sure extra orange is scaled too, as its extra blue is.

### P11: Endless pays like the acts you've cleared, plus milestones
- Kills pay at max(the wave's reward act, acts cleared), capped at 6.
- The **first time** a save reaches waves 10, 20, 30 and 40, it pays 300 / 600 / 900 / 1,200 blue and 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 orange, with a toast or banner.
- The claimed milestones are saved: `SaveGame` with `reset`, `to_dict` and `_apply`, `VERSION` unchanged, and old saves load with none claimed.
- Growth after the build is done (N18) is left for later, as the user said.
- **As built:** A milestone pays when its wave is **won** (as the best wave counts), so a debug jump or dying to the wave-10 boss pays nothing. The amounts are fixed (no tree, modifier or difficulty scaling). It shows as the rising essence icons, a tip banner and a result line. The pay act isn't floored at Act 3; only the pools are.

### P9: Endless bosses keep up with the waves
- Boss HP = max(today's formula, 0.6 × the previous wave's total enemy HP).
- Boss and mini boss damage = max(today's tier power, √(that wave's normal damage scale)).
- Fix B (burns) and B13 (`burn_scale`) follow the new power.
- **As built:** `wave_hp(w)` is the expected HP of a normal wave, so it is deterministic. A follow-up commit anchors the damage at each kind's first wave, max(tier, √(damage_scale(w) / damage_scale(first))), so the first boss and mini boss stay ×1.0. Bosses: 1.0 / 1.27 / 1.54 / 1.81 / 2.08 at waves 10–50. Boss HP at wave 20 is 26,381 with only Acts 1–3 cleared (was 19,500).

### P10: Endless draws only from cleared acts

- Check the act of every id in the pools.

### P12: Pulsefire: ability haste and W damage, no %AP

### P14: PROJECT +8% move speed, and the bot picks a skin
- PROJECT's move speed bonus goes from +15% to +8%.

### P15: One penetration item, and a full backpack takes nothing
- **The rule:** of Lord Dominik's, Mortal Reminder and Void Staff, only one can be in the equip slots. A move, swap or purchase that would equip a second one is refused with a small pop-up: "Only one penetration item can be equipped: <the equipped one>".
- **Shadowflame** becomes 15 flat magic penetration.
- **The V1.4.2 Fix 39 spill is removed:** buys and pickups no longer go into empty equip slots.
  - **Merchant:** buying with every backpack cell full shows a pop-up, "Your backpack is full", and the buy is declined. A drag onto a free equip slot still buys, if the pen rule allows. Potions keep their own cells.
  - **Ground loot:** with the backpack full, Lucian walks over the item and it stays on the ground. Show a short "Backpack full" note at most once every few seconds.
- The bot's shopping and pickup are updated to match.
- **As built:** `ItemData.is_penetration()` is true for a legendary with `armor_pen` or `magic_pen`. `Inventory.pen_blocker` guards every put, and `drop_outcome` refuses a second one. `ConfirmPopup.tell()` is a one-button notice. A shop item dropped on a taken equip slot goes to the backpack. A craft whose first input's equip cell is closed by the rule puts the legendary in the second input's cell; with both closed, it is refused. The bot equips first to make room when loot doesn't fit.

### P16: Defy heals only on elite, mini boss or boss kills
Death's Dance's Defy heal (15% max HP over 2 s) comes only from killing an elite, a mini boss or a boss. The bleed cleanse still comes on any takedown.
- **As built:** A victim that isn't an `EnemyUnit` (the training dummy) clears the bleed but never heals.

### P17: More healers for Grievous Wounds
Every heal goes through `Enemy.heal`, so Grievous Wounds cuts it.
- **Black Mist (Act 5):** a normal or elite enemy inside a bank regenerates 2% of max HP a second while hurt.
- **Maiden of the Mist (Act 5):** heals 50% of the damage her hits deal, and 5% of max HP each time she summons ghouls.
- **Final Thresh (6-5):** heals 3% of max HP each time one of his Lantern Souls reaches him or dies near him. Check how the souls work and pick the fitting trigger.
- Show the heal (a green number or flash, if enemies have one).
- **As built:** Thresh's souls never walk to him, so the trigger is a soul **killed** within 400 of him, capped at 10 a fight (30% of his HP). A new `EnemyUnit.healed` signal shows a green "+N" (gathered over 0.4 s), not while veiled; the Ocean Drake's heals show it too. The Maiden uses two new `EnemyData` fields, `drain` and `summon_heal`.
- **After the bot runs:** the first build walled early Act 5. 5-2 went from 1 to 8 tries for the skilled bot and from 4 to 16 for the average one, with most deaths in wave 1 to the Maiden and her ghouls. The user chose to soften it. The Black Mist regen became 1% a second, only while the enemy is veiled. The Maiden's drain became 25%, and her summon heal 2%. Thresh's harvest is unchanged.
