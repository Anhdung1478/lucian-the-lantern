# V1.2: Evolution Tree, node states, skin purchase confirmation and menus

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Version:** 1.2.0 (from 1.1.0)

## Goal

- **A nicer Evolution Tree:** a living, leafy tree instead of today's bare one, which looks a bit creepy.
- **Clear node states:** "not bought", "in progress" and "done" can be told apart at a glance.
- **A confirmation pop-up** before a skin is bought.
- **A better MENU** for the stage (ESC) and the main screen (the gear button). Today it is plain text buttons in white, navy and grey.

**Out of scope**
- Gameplay and economy. Costs, rules and `EvolutionTree` stay the same.
- The tree page layout. `TreePage.SIZE`, `CELL`, the node centres and the 96 px node size don't move.
- The title screen's buttons.
- The Settings screen.
- The node info column's layout. It only follows the new state colours.

## Where things stand

- **Node states:** `TreeNodeView.state_of` returns hidden, open, poor, bought or maxed.
  - **open:** a gold border (`UiStyle.BORDER_COLOR`).
  - **poor:** a red border.
  - **bought:** a pale-gold border (`ffe9a8`) with a glow.
  - **maxed:** a green border with a glow.
  - Open and bought look almost the same. The only sign of progress is the small "2/5" badge.
- **The tree:** `TreePage._draw` paints a straight grey-brown tapered strip (`_draw_trunk`), bare bezier limbs (`_draw_strip`), faint blob "canopy" and a few small leaves. All the scenery and tree drawing sits in `tree_page.gd`, which is 733 lines.
- **Buying a skin:** `SkinCard.button` → `SkinShop._buy` → `SaveGame.buy_skin` buys at once.
- **The menus:** `GameMenu` (stage ESC) and `GearMenu` (main screen) are both a `UiStyle.panel()` box: a "MENU" label, an `HSeparator` and plain `UiStyle.style_button` buttons. `ConfirmPopup` looks the same.

## Choices

| Topic | Choice |
|---|---|
| Which menus | The **gear menu** on the main screen and the **stage ESC menu**. `ConfirmPopup` gets the same frame. |
| Tree style | **Lush lantern tree.** A gnarled trunk, tapering boughs, layered leaf canopies, little lanterns that light up when a node is bought, and gold light flowing up from the roots. |
| Node states | **Grey → Gold → Green.** |
| Menu look | **An ornate card** over the blurred game. |

## 1. Node states (`TreeNodeView`)

The state ids stay, so the rules and tests keep their meaning. Only the look changes.

| | Hidden | **Not bought** (open, poor) | **In progress** (bought) | **Done** (maxed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Icon | "?" (as today) | **greyscale** (`grey_icon.gdshader`, as the HUD's `SkillSlot` uses) | full colour | full colour |
| Frame | dark, faint border | 2 px **silver** `aeb6c4`, drop shadow, no glow | 3 px **gold** `ffcf5a`, gold glow | 3 px **green** `6fd36f`, green glow |
| Level bar | none | every segment empty | lit gold up to the level | every segment green |
| Badge text | none | "0/5" | "2/5" | **"MAX"** |
| Corner chips | none | **▲ ready** (gold, top-left) while the next level can be bought | ▲ ready while it can be bought; **lock** (bottom-right) while the next level is closed | **✔ crest** (green disc, white check, top-right) |

**Badge and level bar**
- The badge is a pill tucked under the frame (76 × 30 instead of 58 × 28).
- The level number is on top. Along the bottom runs a strip with one segment per level (at most 7).
- `badge` stays a `Label` with the text. A drawn child behind it paints the pill and the segments.

**Too dear**
- A "poor" node no longer has a red frame. It looks not bought and has no ▲ chip.
- The info column's price is red when short, as today.

**Other rules**
- **FREE** stays at the top-right. A free upgrade shows FREE instead of ▲. A maxed node is never free, so FREE and the crest never meet.
- **The info column follows:** the list rows' icons and the portrait are greyscale while a node is not bought. The level colours come from `state_color`, which maps open and poor to silver.
- **Hover and selected outlines:** as today.

## 2. The lush lantern tree (`TreeArt`)

**Code**
- The scenery and the tree drawing move out of `TreePage` into `src/ui/main/tree/tree_art.gd` (`class_name TreeArt extends RefCounted`).
- A page builds one from its layout and the same seeded random numbers, so the drawing never changes between redraws.
- The page's layout fields become public: `limbs`, `parents`, `on_trunk` and `trunk_top`.
- `TreePage` keeps the layout, the node views, the vines, R's ring, the Backpack plaques, the captions and the header.

| Call | Draws | Called from |
|---|---|---|
| `draw_back(canvas)` | Sky, moon, hills, side trees, back canopy | `TreePage._draw` |
| `draw_tree(canvas, tree)` | Roots, trunk, boughs, front canopy, lanterns | `TreePage._draw` |
| `draw_flow(canvas, tree, time)` | Gold light climbing to bought nodes | The motes layer (redrawn each frame while the page shows) |

**Palette**

| Part | Colours |
|---|---|
| Bark | dark `2b1d16`, mid `5a4031`, light `7a5a43` |
| Leaves | deep `123a2c`, mid `1f5a3f`, light `3f8a5c` |
| Moonlight rim (from the upper right) | `9fc4d8` on bark, `8fd4a8` on leaves |
| Lantern light | `MainBackdrop.GROVE_LIGHT` |

**Trunk**
- A polygon between two edge curves.
- A root flare about 150 px wide at the ground, narrowing to about 76 px, then tapering to the top.
- Small bumps on its edges but no sway, so nodes on the centre column still sit on it.
- Darker on the left, with a moonlit rim on the right.
- 5–7 wavy bark grooves, a few highlight strokes and one knot hole away from the nodes.

**Roots**
- 5–7 tapered roots gripping the grassy mound.
- A few small glowing flowers or mushrooms beside them.

**Boughs**
- Today's bezier paths, about 30 px thick at the base.
- They start inside the trunk, and the trunk is drawn over them, so the joints blend.
- Darker underneath, with a rim light on top.
- 1–2 short twigs with leaf sprigs.

**Canopy**
- Overlapping leaf clumps in three tones:
  - a dark back layer;
  - a mid layer;
  - a light front layer with moonlit rim arcs.
- Clumps sit around each node and along each bough, so every limb group reads as one leafy crown.
- None touches the header box (`HEADER_RECT`) or the Reset tree corner.
- Near a node's centre the clumps stay dark. Nodes are drawn on top, so icons and name plates stay readable.

**Lanterns**
- A small lantern (about 14 × 22: cap, cage, glass and flame) hangs on a string from each bough, about 60 % of the way to its node.
- **Lit** (warm glow and flame) once that node has a level. Dark glass before.
- Skipped where it would touch a node, its badge or its name.
- Nodes on the trunk have no bough, so they have no lantern.

**Light flow**
- 2–3 gold motes per bought node climb from the roots, up the trunk and along the boughs to it, at about 60 px/s.
- With Low effects there is no flow.

**Kept**
- The vines (requirement links): dim, gold once bought. Dim vines are a little fainter.
- R's segmented ring, the Backpack plaques, the limb captions, the header, the frame and the drifting motes.

## 3. Skin purchase confirmation (`SkinPurchasePopup`)

`src/ui/main/skins/skin_purchase_popup.gd`, a `Control` child of `SkinShop`, like `SkinPreview`.

**Frame**
- A `MenuCard` (Section 4), titled **PURCHASE SKIN**, with the skin's name as its subtitle.
- Over a `MenuBackdrop`, so the shop behind it is blurred.

**Content**
- Left: the skin's portrait, in a frame lit with its accent (`MainBackdrop.ACCENTS`).
- Right: a table.

| Row | Shows |
|---|---|
| Bonuses | green lines |
| Weaknesses | red lines |
| Price | the cost and the purple icon |
| You have | purple owned and the purple icon |
| After purchase | owned minus cost and the purple icon |

**Under the table**
- A dim note: "Buying does not equip it."
- **Purchase 25** (the main button, gold): buys, plays `coin` and closes. Disabled while the price can't be paid; it follows `essences.changed`.
- **Cancel:** closes and changes nothing.

**Flow**
1. `SkinCard.buy_pressed` → `SkinShop.ask_purchase(id)` opens the pop-up.
2. Its `confirmed` signal → `SaveGame.buy_skin(id)`.

**Closing**
- ESC closes it through `SkinShop.close_popup()`, as it closes the preview.
- A hidden shop closes it.
- The preview and the purchase pop-up replace each other.
- Nothing is saved to file.

## 4. Menus (ornate card)

### Shared pieces (`src/ui/menu/`)

| File | Class | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `menu_backdrop.gd`, `menu_backdrop.gdshader` | `MenuBackdrop` | Full-rect: blurs what is behind it (a fixed 13-tap blur of the screen texture, no mipmaps, so any renderer works), darkens it and adds a vignette |
| `menu_card.gd` | `MenuCard` | The ornate card (below) |
| `menu_entry.gd` | `MenuEntry` | One menu row: an icon, the label and an optional hint |

**`MenuCard`**
- A drop shadow and a vertical gradient fill, navy `0b1220` → deep teal `0d2328`.
- A double gold frame: 2 px outside, 1 px inside at a 6 px inset.
- Diamond ornaments at the corners.
- A round medallion with the lantern (`title_lantern.svg`) over the top edge, in a warm glow.
- A letter-spaced title, and a subtitle between two short gold rules.
- API: `set_title()`, `set_subtitle()` and `content` (a `VBoxContainer`).

**`MenuEntry`**
- A `Button`, about 380 × 58. `text` is the label, so `find_button(text)` keeps working.
- A tinted white icon on the left; a dim hint on the right.
- Kinds:

| Kind | Normal | Hover |
|---|---|---|
| **PRIMARY** | Gold fill and border | Brighter |
| **NORMAL** | Navy, faint gold border | A 4 px gold bar on the left; gold border, text and icon |
| **DANGER** | As NORMAL | Red bar, border and icon |

- **Disabled:** dim, with its `hover_text` note as today.
- The click sound is `UiStyle`'s, connected once.

**Icons:** new white SVGs, 32 × 32, tinted by the entry: `assets/art/ui/menu_resume.svg`, `menu_close`, `menu_save`, `menu_copy`, `menu_settings`, `menu_leave`, `menu_exit`, `menu_back`.

### The menus

**Stage ESC menu (`GameMenu`)**
- Subtitle: the run's name ("Stage 2-3", "Training"). `Stage._ready` sets it.

| Row | Kind | Hint |

| Leave this stage | DANGER | |

**Main screen gear menu (`GearMenu`)**
- Subtitle: "Slot N · <play time>".

| Row | Kind | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Close | PRIMARY | |

**`ConfirmPopup`**
- Gets the `MenuCard` frame and the `MenuBackdrop`.
- Its buttons, behaviour and API don't change.
