# V1.4.1 report: crafting at the merchant, and eleven review fixes

**Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Version:** 1.4.1 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v1.4.1.md` · **Branch:** `v1.4.1` (from `v1.4`)

## Summary

V1.4.1 fixes the twelve review items you picked, one commit each, in the order you asked for: 1, 13, 12, 14, 11, 15, 17, 18, 21, 22, 23, 24.

- **The big one is bug 1:** crafting then selling made gold. Crafting has moved from the backpack to a new **Craft** tab at the merchant, for a fee. The fee is the legendary's cost minus its two fragments' costs.
- **The other eleven are small bugs.** Their tests and docs are updated with them.

The full suite passes: **1828 tests**, up from 1804 (see [Tests](#tests)).

The fee makes the story harder. As you asked, I measured it with the bots and **did not retune anything**: see [Bots](#bots).

### The Craft tab (bug 1)

| | Two fragments | The boots and a fragment |
|---|---|---|
| Makes | Their legendary | The upgraded boots |
| Fee | Legendary cost − the two fragments' costs | The boots' `upgrade_cost`, as in V1.4 |
| Example | Tear + Tear → Muramana: 2900 − 400 − 400 = **2100** | Boots of Speed + Recurve Bow → Berserker's Greaves: **300** |
| Lands in | The **first** input's cell (an equipped fragment stays equipped) | The boots cell |

- **Crafting then selling never pays.** Selling the crafted legendary returns 0.7 × cost − fee, which is always less than selling the two fragments, by 0.3 × (cost − fragments). A test checks all 33 recipes.
- **The same price either way.** Crafting a legendary costs the same as buying it.
- **The inputs:**
  - Each takes a fragment or the boots, in either order.
  - Anything else is refused: "Only fragments and boots are crafted".
  - A right-click empties an input, and closing the shop empties both.
- **Craft** stays off until the two inputs make something. The fee shows red when you can't pay it, and pressing Craft then says "Not enough gold".
- The note line walks through it:
  - "Drag in two fragments, or your boots and a fragment."
  - "Now drag in a fragment."
  - "X + Y → Z"
  - "No legendary is made from X and Y."
  - "X doesn't upgrade …"
- **The recipe table stays** beside the backpack while the shop is open, since crafting happens there.
- **Cards:**
  - A fragment's card says "Craft it with another fragment at the merchant's Craft tab."
  - A legendary's card says "Recipe: X + Y + N gold at the merchant."

### Choices worth knowing

- **Bug 1:**
  - The Boots tab is replaced, not kept beside a new tab, because one Craft tab now does both.
  - The class `UpgradeSlot` is now `CraftSlot`, and the shop result `NO_RECIPE` replaces `NOT_UPGRADABLE` and `NO_BOOTS`.
- **Bug 11:**
  - A save from before the flag counts as not seen. A save hit by the bug gets The End on its next 6-5 clear.
  - A save that already saw it gets it once more.
- **Bug 13:** A right-click on a potion in the backpack now does nothing.
- **Bug 17:** A shot of Senna's that lands after Lucian has died gives him nothing.
- **Bug 18:**
  - `Lucian.skill_haste(slot)` is the one formula for both the card and the cast. It includes Malignance's R haste.
  - Buff timers on hovered buff cards still freeze; they weren't in scope. Any card can opt in with `tooltip_version()`.
- **Bug 21:** A slot whose main file is broken but whose backup reads is **not** broken: it loads the backup, as before.
- **Bug 24:** With some essence spent, Reset still clears the free levels too, as before. They show FREE again.

## 6-5 balance check (bug 12)

**Setup:** 6-5 alone on Normal, 3 runs for each bot and each side.

- **Starting essence:** 12,900 blue and 90 orange. That is what the baseline skilled bot had earned on reaching 6-5. It was read from its save, minus what 6-5 itself paid.
- **Before:** the tree with fixes 1 and 13, and without 12.
- **After:** the final tree.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Skilled: tries per run | 1, 1, 1 | 1, 1, 1 |
| Average: tries per run | 4, 3, 4 | 6, 1, 1 |
| Average: deaths in the boss wave | 2 | 0 |

- **No change is needed.** The plan's rule was to tune Dark Passage only if the median tries rose by more than 1, or the average bot walled. Neither happened.
- **Where the deaths are:** most of the average bot's deaths are in the early waves, where 8–12 ordinary Lantern Souls a wave do most of the damage. Those souls were never affected.
- **Dark Passage is unchanged:** 3 souls, a cap of 6, every 16 s.

## Bots

**What was measured:** the whole story from a new save on Normal, with the headless bot (`tests/tools/autoplay.gd`).

- **Before:** V1.4 (`270dd96`), with free crafting in the backpack.
- **After:** the final tree, with crafting at the merchant for a fee.
- **The bot's crafting:** before, it combined every pair it held for free. After, it crafts at the merchant whenever the fee leaves it 100 gold.
- **No retune:** nothing else changed, as you asked.

| | Skilled before | Skilled after | Average before | Average after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Story time** (game time) | **3 h 57** | **4 h 38** (+41 min) | **6 h 26** | **8 h 34** (+2 h 08) |
| Act 3 | 56 min | 49 min | 1 h 44 | **3 h 43** |
| Act 5 | 1 h 36 | **2 h 22** | 2 h 30 | 2 h 03 |
| Act 6 | 46 min | 50 min | 1 h 36 | 2 h 16 |
| Tries, all stages | 32 | 34 | 56 | 73 |
| Farming replays | 2 | 2 | 10 | 13 |
| Crafts | 111 | 68 | 167 | 140 |
| Gold paid in fees | — | 81,000 | — | 169,050 |

**Stages that took the most tries:**

| | Skilled before | Skilled after | Average before | Average after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-2 | 8 | 6 | 10 | 11 |
| 3-5 | 1 | 1 | 7 | **19** |
| 5-2 | 2 | **5** | 1 | 6 |
| 5-4 | 1 | 1 | 7 | 2 |
| 6-5 | 1 | 1 | 10 | 9 |

**What this says:**

- **The fee makes the story longer, and harder for the weaker bot.**
  - Skilled: +17%. That moves it toward the 5–7 h target (it was below it).
  - Average: +33%. Most of that is the Elder Dragon's 3-5: 19 tries against 7.
  - With the 25-try cap no stage walled. With the usual 10-try cap, the average bot walls at 3-2 both before (V1.4's own run) and after, and after the fee it would also wall at 3-5.
- **Fewer legendaries.** The bots craft about 40% (skilled) and 16% (average) fewer, and spend the gold on fees instead.
- **The bots vary.** The same numbers can give a stage 1 try or 10. Read the times as ±30–60 minutes, as in V1.3.

**If you want to soften it** (not done, your call), here are three options:
- More kill gold in Acts 3–6.
- A lower fee: for example, a share of (cost − fragments).
- A cheaper Elder Dragon stage.

The bot can measure any of them.
