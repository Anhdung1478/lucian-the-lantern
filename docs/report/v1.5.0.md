# V1.5.0 report: balance pass, the open items and a synthesized score

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Version:** 1.5.0 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v1.5.0.md` · **Branch:** `v1.5.0` (from `v1.4.3`)

## Summary

V1.5.0 takes the review's balance findings, three of the four items still open from V1.3.0, the synthesized score, and your fragment rework. There is one commit per item, in this order: 16, 19, 31, N25, P1, P3, P4, P5, P6, P8, P11, P9, P10, P12, P13, P14, P15, P16, P17, P18, P7. P9 has a follow-up commit; two review-fix commits and Pulsefire's 25 ability haste (your follow-up) come at the end. Item 20 was skipped, as you asked.

- **The walls are softer.** The Infernal Drake, the Elder Dragon and Maokai have less HP. Act 3 pays 70% more kill gold, and every act's second stage has 90% HP normals.
- **Progress is smoother:**
  - skill ranks 2–3 cost less, and R's rank 1 is a free claim after Act 2;
  - orange grows with the act and with Hard and Extreme;
  - Backpack Slots 5–7 cost less orange;
  - fragments cost 650–1,100, so craft fees even out at 900–1,600.
- **Endless:**
  - It pays like the acts you've cleared, with first-time payouts at waves 10, 20, 30 and 40.
  - Its bosses keep up with the waves.
  - It draws only from cleared acts.
- **Items and skins:**
  - Only one penetration item can be equipped, and a full backpack takes nothing.
  - Shadowflame's penetration is flat.
  - Defy heals only on elite or boss kills, and Smite heals only on a kill.
  - Pulsefire and PROJECT are reworked.
  - Shieldbow counts only shields that would absorb the hit.
- **Enemies heal:** the Black Mist, the Maiden and final Thresh, so Grievous Wounds has targets.
- **The rest:**
  - The game has music: a synthesized score for each act that follows the fight.
  - The debug tables open only in debug builds.
  - The Journey opens on the latest open stage.

The full suite passes except the known flaky raptor test: **2158 of 2159**, up from 2015. A review of the whole branch found no serious game bugs, and its findings are fixed. The bots finish the story with no wall, but the skilled bot is now **under the 5–7 h target** (see [Bots](#bots)).

## The score

All music is made in code at runtime by `src/audio/score.gd` and played by `src/audio/music.gd`, a node the Game owns, on the Music bus. There are no audio files.

| Piece | Mode | Tempo | Loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theme (title, main screen, The End, Training) | D major | 66 | 8 bars |
| Act 1, The Loss | A minor | 60 | 8 bars |
| Act 2, The Sentinels | E Mixolydian | 96 | 12 bars |
| Act 3, The Jungle | C Dorian | 104 | 12 bars |
| Act 4, The Darkness | B Phrygian | 76 | 8 bars |
| Act 5, Shadow Isles | F Locrian | 70 | 8 bars |
| Act 6, The Lantern | D harmonic minor | 126 | 16 bars |

- **Layers:** five, played in sync.
  - A drone.
  - A pulse that grows with the enemies alive (full at 12).
  - Lucian's voice.
  - Senna's voice.
  - A boss layer, from a boss or mini boss appearing until its wave ends.
- **Between waves and in cutscenes:** the pulse and boss layers fade out. On defeat only the drone stays.
- **Senna's voice:** heard in Act 1, gone after it, and back once 6-5 is cleared.
- **Transitions:** layers fade over 2 s, pieces crossfade over 3 s, and pauses play it 6 dB quieter.
- **Cost:** each piece renders once on worker threads (about 0.2–0.7 s), and at most three are kept (about 10 MB). Headless runs render nothing.
- **Not heard yet:** nobody has listened to it; every choice was checked by numbers. On first play, listen for the balance against the sound effects (`Score.LAYER_PEAKS`), Act 4's and Act 5's harsh drones, Act 1's low drone on laptop speakers, and the fade lengths.
