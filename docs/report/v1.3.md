# V1.3 report: a playtest, a difficulty pass, difficulty levels and design advice

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Version:** 1.3.0 · **Design:** `lucian_game_design.md` §17

## Summary

I played the story the way a player would. A bot drove Lucian through every stage, headless (no window, no mouse). On 1.2's numbers the game was too hard:

- **A new player could not finish the tutorial.** Standing and shooting, Lucian died in 26 s, in wave 1, 10 times out of 10.
- **An average player was stuck at the first stage of Act 2.** 2-1 was lost 10 times out of 10, mostly in wave 1.
- **A skilled player** needed 7 tries for 2-1 and 7 for 2-3, spent 1 h 23 min in Act 2, and was then stuck at 3-1: dead within 30 s in all 10 tries, with 0–2 kills.

The root cause was structural, not one bad enemy. Lucian restarted every stage at level 1 while enemies grew ×1.6 to ×4.0 per act, so each act's first wave was the hardest fight of the game. Skill ranks bought in the Evolution Tree could not be used either, because the levels they need were never reached.

After the pass (all changes in `lucian_game_design.md` §17):

- **Novice:** clears the tutorial. They still can't survive Act 2 without moving, even on Easy (see below).
- **Average player:** plays through the story on Normal in 4 h 12 of play. Most stages fall on the first or second try; the hardest take 5–6.
- **Skilled player:** plays through it in 3 h 16, and through **Hard** in 5 h 24.

V1.3 also adds **four difficulty levels**: Easy ×0.8, Normal ×1.0, Hard ×1.1 and Extreme ×1.3, with blue essence ×1.2 on Hard and ×1.5 on Extreme. Each level now fits a kind of player: Easy is a story mode, Normal is for the average player, and Hard is for the skilled one, at about **6 h** for a person: inside the 5–7 h target (see [Length](#length)). Extreme is beyond the skilled bot: it stops at 3-2 (see [Difficulty](#difficulty)).

## How I played

The bot plays the real game: the real `Stage`, `Lucian`, enemies, merchant and Evolution Tree. It is headless at a fixed 60 fps, about 8× faster than real time. Between stages it spends essences in a sensible order: Q, W and the Passive first, then Health, AD, Heal and equip slots, saving up for the next one. In a stage it:

- shoots the weakest enemy in range, the melee ones near it first
- casts Q through the line with the most enemies, W on the thickest clump, R on a boss or a crowd, and E to escape or to trigger the passive
- steps out of red warnings, and sidesteps bolts (some tiers)
- kites: it steps back from melee enemies while reloading
- drinks a potion under 55% HP, and uses Heal or Barrier under 35%
- at each break, picks up loot, buys the best item it can afford plus up to 4 potions, crafts any two fragments, fills the equip slots, and presses Skip

A lost stage is retried, keeping the essences. After 3 losses it replays the last cleared stage once to farm. More than 10 losses is a **wall**.

| Tier | Reaction | Kites | Steps out of red warnings | Sidesteps bolts | E for damage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skilled | 0.15 s | Yes, 90 px outside a swing | 100% | 50% | Yes |
| Average | 0.25 s | Yes, 40 px outside a swing | 80% | 20% | No |
| Novice | 0.35 s | Never | 50% | Never | No |

A bot is not a person. It sees everything and never panics, but it aims and positions worse than a good player. Read the tiers as reference points, not as exact players.

Same bot, same rules, on an untouched copy of the game:

| Tier | Tries per stage | Game time | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1-1: 10 lost | 4 min | **Wall at 1-1.** Dead in wave 1 every time, with 2–4 kills. |
| Average | 1-1: 1 · 2-1: 10 lost | 21 min | **Wall at 2-1.** The siege minion's shells (7 deaths) and the super minion (3). |
| Skilled | 1-1: 1 · 2-1: 7 · 2-2: 2 · 2-3: 7 · 3-1: 10 lost | 1 h 33 min | **Wall at 3-1.** Act 2 alone took 1 h 23 min. In 3-1, dead within 30 s with 0–2 kills, all 10 times. |

What a trace of those fights shows:

- **Lucian barely levels.** He ends 2-1 at level 3, Act 3 at 3–6 and Act 6 at 10–14. Rank 3 of a skill needs level 5 and R needs level 6, so the ranks bought in the tree mostly sat unused.
- **1-1 → 2-1 is a cliff.** 2-1 holds 12.7k HP against the tutorial's 4.7k, with no Senna, and the player can buy at most one skill rank with what the tutorial pays.
- **Act 3's monsters outrun him.** Raptors run at 330 and wolves at 340, against Lucian's 345, while his shots stop him. A pack of 8 wraps him at level 1, and kiting, Lucian's whole kit, does nothing.
- **Sticky elites.** One hit from the Blue Sentinel slowed Lucian 30%, to 241, the Sentinel's own speed: he could never get away again. Super minions were the top killer in Act 2.
- **Mana runs dry.** In most traces his mana sat at 0–60, so Q and W were off half the fight.

## What changed

See `lucian_game_design.md` §17 for the full table. In short:

1. **Lucian starts each story stage at his act's level: 1, 2, 5, 5, 7, 9** (your 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9 with Act 3 raised to 5 after the Hard playtest; I first tried 1, 3, 6, 7, 9, 11). He also levels faster: the next level needs 100 + 60 × level XP (was 180 + 100 × level). Act 1, Training and Endless still start at level 1.
2. **Gentler growth:** enemy HP per act 0.6 → 0.25, damage per act 0.25 → 0.15, growth per wave 15% → 10%. Rewards per act are unchanged.
3. **The first stage of every act is gentle:** −20% HP for its normal enemies. 3-1's wave 2 has one raptor pack, not two.
4. **The tutorial:** a smaller first wave (4 melee and 2 ranged, was 6 and 4), 4 ranged in wave 2 (was 6), and minions that hit 25% softer.
5. **Swarms run slower than Lucian:** raptors 300, wolves 310, spiderlings 330, saplings 320, Mist Walkers 320.
6. **Act 3:**
   - The small monsters have about 22% less HP and 25% less damage.
   - The Blue Sentinel and Red Brambleback have 1400 HP and 30 / 34 damage (the Sentinel's 36 became 30 after the Hard playtest).
   - The Blue Sentinel's slow is 20% for 1 s.
   - A drake's wave brings two packs instead of four.
7. **Super minions:** 1150 HP and 36 damage (were 1400 and 45).
8. **A soft-lock fixed.** Bots fought the Ocean Drake for **35 and 40 minutes**: below ~200 damage a second, its Soothing Mist healed more than he dealt. It now heals 20% (was 30%), 5% of its HP interrupts it (was 8%), and it channels at most 3 times a fight.
9. **Mana regen** 14 → 20 per 5 s.
10. **Difficulty levels:** Easy ×0.8, Normal ×1.0, Hard ×1.1, Extreme ×1.3 on every enemy's HP and on all the damage Lucian takes, with blue essence ×1.2 on Hard and ×1.5 on Extreme. Picked with a new game, changed from the gear menu. See [Difficulty](#difficulty).

Bosses and mini bosses keep their numbers (they never scaled), and Endless keeps its own scaling.

The **novice** (never moves except for some red warnings) clears the tutorial on its second try. It still loses 2-1 every time, usually within 30 s, to the ranged minions it never steps away from. I left that alone: a player who never moves can't play an action game, and the tutorial now teaches moving before it is needed.

What the tries say:

- **No walls on Normal.** The worst stages took 5–6 tries, with farming in between: 3-2 and 3-5 for the average bot, 3-5 for the skilled one (the Elder Dragon's stage).
- **Act 3 is still the hardest act.** Acts 2 and 4 are mostly first-try clears. See advice 8.
- **No single killer.** At 30 damage the Blue Sentinel no longer leads; the average bot's deaths are spread over the Red Brambleback (4), the Elder Dragon (4), super minions (3) and others.

## Length

The design's budget is **323–420 minutes (5.4–7 h)** for a skilled player's first clear, counting deaths, shopping and the replays to afford tree nodes (§1).

Minutes of play, replays to farm included, with the final numbers:

| Act | Budget | Skilled bot, Normal | Average bot, Normal | Skilled bot, **Hard** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8–10 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 2 | 30–40 | 11 | 26 | 16 |
| 3 | 70–90 | 54 | 1 h 17 | 1 h 09 |
| 4 | 45–60 | 20 | 21 | 30 |
| 5 | 80–100 | 51 | 1 h 16 | 1 h 20 |
| 6 | 90–120 | 58 | 51 | 2 h 07 |
| **All** | **5.4–7 h** | **3 h 16** | **4 h 12** | **5 h 24** |

The bot's minutes are stage clocks: fighting only, as the end screen counts them. A person also shops (the budget allows about 30 s a break, and the story has about 100 breaks: some 50 minutes), watches the cutscenes and reads the tree. So:

- **On Hard, a skilled person should need about 6¼ h: inside the target**, and the skilled bot's Acts 3, 5 and 6 sit right on their budgets. Only Acts 2 and 4 run short.
- **On Normal,** a skilled person needs about 4–4½ h and an average one about 5 h.

That is the split advice 1 suggested: the 5–7 h target lives on Hard, and Normal is a shorter, friendlier story.

During the first try (levels 1, 3, 6, 7, 9, 11), a 40-minute replay of 3-4 stalled on the Ocean Drake. That is how the last soft-lock was found: the drake now channels at most 3 times a fight, and a re-check with a weak Lucian cleared 3-4 in 7:44.

Before the pass, the skilled bot needed 1 h 23 min for Act 2 alone, but only by dying: 13 losses in 3 stages. That kind of length is the kind your Act 1 note asked to avoid. If you want more hours later, get them from content, as that note says, not from harder numbers:

- one more wave in Acts 2 and 4 (their stages are the shortest)
- first-clear goals that bring players back (clear 3-3 without a potion, beat Akshan without dying), paid in purple

## Difficulty

Four levels, picked after the slot in Start new game and changed any time from the gear menu (`lucian_game_design.md` §17):

| Difficulty | Enemy HP | Damage Lucian takes | Blue essence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | ×0.8 | ×0.8 | ×1.0 |
| **Normal** | ×1.0 | ×1.0 | ×1.0 |
| Hard | ×1.1 | ×1.1 | ×1.2 |
| Extreme | ×1.3 | ×1.3 | ×1.5 |

The HP covers every enemy, bosses too, and the damage covers every hit (boss skills, hazards and burns included). Gold, XP, orange and purple don't change.

### First round: your first numbers

Hard ×1.2 and Extreme ×1.5, no extra blue, Act 3 at level 4, the Blue Sentinel at 36. Easy and Normal played well, but **Hard and Extreme stopped both bots at the start of Act 3**: at 3-2 on Hard and 3-1 on Extreme. The Blue Sentinel dealt the killing blow in 8 of the skilled bot's 10 lost tries of 3-2 on Hard and in 19 of 20 lost tries of 3-1 on Extreme, in wave 2, with Lucian at level 4–5. Losing didn't make him stronger: a lost stage paid 40–60 blue, and on Hard even the farming replays failed. You chose all four fixes I offered: Hard ×1.1 and Extreme ×1.3, blue ×1.2 and ×1.5 on them, Act 3 at level 5, and the Sentinel at 30 damage.

### Final numbers

The bots on each level (10 lost tries on one stage = stuck):

| | Novice | Average | Skilled |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Easy** | stuck at 2-2 \* | **cleared**, 2 h 52 | not run |
| **Normal** | stuck at 2-1 \* | **cleared**, 4 h 12 | **cleared**, 3 h 16 |
| **Hard** | not run | stuck at 3-2 | **cleared**, 5 h 24 |
| **Extreme** | not run | stuck at 3-1 | stuck at 3-2 |

\* From the first round. The novice stops in Act 2, before any of the four changes matter.

- **Easy is a story mode.** The average bot cleared every stage with no more than 3 tries, without farming once.
- **Hard is for the skilled player.** The skilled bot cleared it with at most 5 tries a stage and 7 farming replays. The average bot stopped at 3-2, but it was getting there: its last tries reached the drake's wave (3 of 4). A patient player who farms will pass.
- **Extreme is beyond the skilled bot.** It loses 3-2 in wave 2, the swarm wave, to the Sentinel (4 of 10), raptors and wolves. A 3-1 replay pays it about 160 blue, so its tree grows slowly; it had R only by its 7th try. A player who kites better than the bot, or farms longer, may get through. If you want the skilled bot to clear Extreme too, the smallest step is more blue there (×2), or Extreme ×1.25.
- **Nobody is locked out:** a stuck player can switch level from the gear menu and back later.

## Advice: what I would improve or redesign

Ordered by how much I think each would help.

### 1. Add a difficulty choice (done in V1.3)
One number scales enemy HP and damage, chosen with the save slot and changeable on the main screen. **Built** with your levels, Easy ×0.8, Normal ×1.0, Hard ×1.1 and Extreme ×1.3 (more blue on the last two); see [Difficulty](#difficulty) for how they play.

### 2. Make items matter earlier
Crafting is a headline feature, but before Act 4 it hardly happens:

- A fragment costs 600–1300 gold, while a whole Act 2 stage pays 2–3.6k.
- Only one equip slot is open until the tree buys more.
- Everything is lost when the stage ends.

So a whole early stage buys about one fragment and some potions. Any of these would help:

- a starting purse per act (for example 300 × act)
- cheaper fragments
- two equip slots from the start
- one item carried between the stages of an act

### 3. Give potions their own key
Potions compete for the one equip slot with real items. The other way to drink is from the backpack, which pauses the game. A dedicated potion slot (or a "drink" key that uses the first potion anywhere in the backpack) removes the choice between surviving and having an item.

### 4. Tell the player what killed them
The single most useful thing in this playtest was the bot's "died to … / taken by …" line: super minions in Act 2, the Blue Sentinel in Act 3. The DEFEATED screen could show **"Killed by: Super Minion"** and the three enemies that dealt the most damage. Players learn what to respect instead of guessing.

### 5. Auto-level the starting skill points
Lucian now starts Act 6 with 9 points to click. Add a setting **"Auto-level skills"**, on by default: R, then the bought ranks of Q, W and E in a set order. Also auto-spend the starting points even with it off, and let level-ups mid-stage stay manual.

### 6. A gentler essence curve early
The skilled bot had earned about 560 blue by the end of Act 2, against nodes of 100–350, so the tree barely moves in the first hour, and a lost stage pays only ~40 blue. Ideas:

- a small "consolation" on defeat (for example +50% of the essences collected)
- cheaper skill ranks 2–3 (200 / 350 → 150 / 250)

### 7. No boss should be able to stall a fight
The Ocean Drake soft-lock was found only because a bot was patient. It is fixed now (3 channels a fight at most). Keep the rule for every future boss: a heal or break mechanic must be beatable at the damage the stage expects, or be limited in number. Karthus's Requiem (8% of 20,000 HP in 3 s to break, else 900 magic) is not a stall, but most players will never break it. Make sure the hit is survivable at Act 5's levels, or lower the break to 5% like the drake's.

### 8. Smooth the act-to-act curve
Act 3 (swarms) is still the hardest act, and Act 4 (slow minions) is the easiest after the pass. Consider:

- giving Act 4 one new twist (a shadow elite that blinks, or shadow pools that slow)
- putting Act 3's drake stages one later (3-3 and 3-5)

### 9. Readability
From the screenshots and the fights:

- In a crowd the enemy HP bars stack into a red wall. Hide full-HP bars of normal enemies until they are hit, or make them thinner.
- Act 5's veiled enemies are dark on a dark map, and Act 6's grey palette is low on contrast. A thin rim on enemies would help.
- Ranged minions outrange Lucian (550 against 500) and Siege 700: say so on their tooltip, so players know why they get hit.

### 10. Skip and cooldowns
Skip gives the break's regen at once, but cooldowns stand still, so whether to wait depends on Ghost, Heal or R. Show the longest summoner or R cooldown next to Skip ("Heal in 0:12").
