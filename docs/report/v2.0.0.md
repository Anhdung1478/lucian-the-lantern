# V2.0.0 report: one look for the whole game, a fairer curve, and fixes

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Version:** 2.0.0 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v2.0.0.md` · **Branch:** `v2.0.0` (from `v1.5.0`, `cd0a495`)

## Summary

V2.0 is the loop you asked for on 2026-11-11: design most features again for a better view, balance again for a skilled player at 4–5 hours and a novice at 7–10, and fix every bug met on the way. It is 92 signed commits on `v2.0.0`. Nothing is pushed.

- **The look.** Every stage screen and every map now matches the title and the Journey: navy plates with gold trim and the lantern's teal light. The HUD, the backpack, the merchant, the end screens, all eleven map variants, the terrain pieces, the units and the combat effects were redrawn. No wall, hitbox, timing or rule changed for the look.
- **Three new rules:**
  - **Resolve:** a stage that beats you gets a little easier each time, so every player gets through.
  - **Next-wave preview:** the break shows what the next wave brings.
  - **Death recap:** the defeat screen says what killed you, with one tip.
- **Balance.** The story has 143 waves (was 106), a cap on summons, more boss health and a little more enemy health in the later acts. On the final numbers the skilled bot finishes in **4 h 13 and 4 h 40** of play, the average bot in 5 h 32 and the novice bot in **8 h 20 and 8 h 37**. No bot hits a wall.
- **Bugs.** A branch review found one real bug in the new code, and four more turned up on screenshots and in the tools. All are fixed with tests.
- **Tests.** The full suite passes: **2432 of 2432**, up from 2159 at V1.5.0.
- **Not published.** The zip is built and smoke-run. It waits for your go.

## Choices worth knowing

Your answers on 2026-11-11: redesign all four areas; match the title and the Journey; an off-screen window for screenshots; build and wait for your go. You also asked me not to run every sub-agent on the most expensive model, so from round 1's restart on, the building was done on cheaper models, with the strongest ones kept for the map art kit and the branch review.

What I decided for you:

- **Resolve is a new rule.** I added it because no number change let the novice through 2-1 without making the game trivial for everyone else. It is temporary (it ends when the stage is cleared), it never applies to a cleared stage, Training or Endless, and it can be turned off. Say so if you don't want it.
- **The summon cap changes Act 5.** Before, the skilled bot needed 744 kills and 25 minutes for 5-5 and lost 5-4 seven times in a row. A player could also farm a quarter of each summon's gold and XP for ever.
- **What "novice" means.** With Resolve, how well the bot dodges barely changes its stage time, because its deaths are quick. What matters is how fast it kills. So the novice tier now wastes 25% of its fight decisions on an aimless step, never kites, backs away only below 25% health, misses half the warnings and every bolt. That is my definition; the times below depend on it.
- **"Play time" is an estimate.** The game's own story clock also runs on the end screen and the main screen. The bot adds 45 s (skilled), 75 s (average) or 120 s (novice) for each visit to the main screen and 10, 15 or 20 s for each Retry.
- **More waves, then a little more health.** More waves alone had diminishing returns: longer stages pay more gold and levels, so Lucian gets stronger. The last 10% came from enemy health per act, which adds 8% in Act 2 and 22% in Act 6, so the early game stays gentle.
- **Boss health follows the gold.** Each boss's health ÷ the gold earned before it is now between 0.67 and 1.88 (V1.5.0: 0.69 to 2.01), so no boss became a wall.
- **The tutorial has Resolve too.** A lost tutorial is still GAME OVER, and the next try gets a stack.
- **The End** hides Thresh and keeps his broken chain on the ground.

|---|---|---|

## What went wrong on the way

- **The screenshot tool, 2026-11-11.** My first off-screen runs re-centred the tool's window on your screen and warped the real mouse for about five minutes. I had checked the code for mouse confinement but not for window moves or cursor warps. The tool now reads a pretend mouse, cannot move its window, and stops if the window leaves its off-screen spot; a watcher confirmed no focus change and no cursor jump over a full run.
- **A sub-agent's slip, 2026-11-12 about 02:10.** One engineer ran Godot once without the headless flag for up to two minutes. A game window with sound may have shown.
- **The usage limit.** Round 1's first six engineers all stopped at the 5-hour limit. Their work was saved in their worktrees, and I restarted the unfinished items on cheaper models.

## Tests

- **Full suite, final:** **2432 of 2432 passing** at `96b33ce`, up from 2159 at V1.5.0. There are 133 scripts and 91,335 asserts, and the run took 897 s on an idle machine. There were no script errors. The log has 32 warning lines, all from tests that make a save or a settings write fail on purpose or name a boss that doesn't exist.
  - **The flaky raptor test is fixed.** `test_swarm_raptors_spread_around_lucian` failed in three of the four earlier full runs and passed alone. The cause was test order, not load: a raptor's spot round Lucian counts every raptor spawned before it in the whole run, so after other tests the pack started from an arrangement that can jam. Each test in that file now starts the count at zero and waits in game time.
- **Earlier full runs:** 2170 of 2170 at the round-1 base; 2381 of 2382 after round 1; 2431 of 2432 with wave plan 2 and the review fixes, and again on the final numbers. Each single failure was that raptor test.
- **Per item:** each engineer ran the test files covering what they touched before each commit, and I ran the covering files again on the merged branch after every merge.
- **Branch review:** one reviewer read the whole V2.0 diff of the game code (147 files, 7,436 lines added). It found the recap bug and two low findings, all fixed, and found the rest clean: Resolve, saves (a V1.5.0 save loads), the preview, the summon cap, input and pause handling, and the maps' geometry, which is byte-identical to V1.5.0.
- **Screenshots:** all 110 scenarios were captured before the work, and the affected ones after each merge. The 23 images in `docs/report/v2.0.0/` are from the final code.

## Not done

- **Hard, Extreme and Easy were not measured** on the new numbers. Hard has half of Resolve and Extreme none, so they may now be harder than before by more than their old share.
- **Endless was not measured.** Its own scaling is unchanged, and its bosses' health doesn't use the story's, but the summon cap and the new units' look apply there too.
- **The Evolution Tree's economy was not retuned.** The novice bot ended with over 12,000 unspent blue essence: its buy list ran out, and orange is what holds the last ranks back. A player who dies a lot will have more blue than the tree can use.
- **Screen fades and motion can't be seen in a still.** They are tested, not watched.
- **The score still hasn't been heard by anyone.**
- **Playing by hand** is yours. Worth a look:
  1. A fight on each act's map: do the units and the red warnings stay clear on the new ground?
  2. Die on purpose at 2-1: the recap, the Resolve line, then the buff on the next try.
  3. A break: the "NEXT" plate, then the wave banner.
  4. The merchant: buy, sell, undo, craft, with the new panel.
  5. Act 5: a Broodmother stops calling spiderlings after ten.
  6. Low quality in Settings: the extras go, the game stays readable.
  7. The end of 6-5: the cutscene, the end card, The End.
