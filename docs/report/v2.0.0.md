# V2.0.0 report: one look for the whole game, a fairer curve, and fixes

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Version:** 2.0.0 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v2.0.0.md` · **Branch:** `v2.0.0` (from `v1.5.0`, `cd0a495`)

## Summary

V2.0 is the loop you asked for on 2026-11-11: design most features again for a better view, balance again for a skilled player at 4–5 hours and a novice at 7–10, and fix every bug met on the way. It was built in four rounds and is 161 signed commits on `v2.0.0`. Nothing is pushed.

- **The look.** Every stage screen and every map matches the title and the Journey: navy plates with gold trim and the lantern's teal light. In the third round the pictures themselves were drawn again: the title, the Journey map, all 38 enemy and boss sprites, the merchant and Senna, Lucian's six bodies and five skin portraits, and Classic's skill effects. In the fourth round all 59 item icons and all 20 stat icons were drawn again, and the recipe table gained the boots' recipes. No wall, hitbox or timing changed for the look.
- **Six new rules:**
  - **Resolve:** a stage that beats you gets a little easier each time, so every player gets through.
  - **Next-wave preview:** the break shows what the next wave brings.
  - **Death recap:** the defeat screen says what killed you, with one tip.
  - **Elite traits** (round 3): from Act 3 each elite is Swift, Armored, Vampiric or Volatile.
  - **The Lantern's gift** (round 3): halfway through a stage you pick one of three boons.
  - **Essence forge** (round 3): 120 blue essence makes 1 orange in the Evolution Tree.
- **Balance.** The story has 143 waves (was 106), a cap on summons, more boss health and a little more enemy health in the later acts. On the final rules the skilled bot finishes in **4 h 23 and 4 h 54** of play, the average bot in 5 h 29 and the novice bot in **6 h 43 and 7 h 34**. The novice is now at the low end of your 7–10 hours: the gift saves a weak player many late defeats. No bot hits a wall on Normal, Hard or Easy.
- **Hard, Extreme, Easy and Endless** were measured for the first time. Two things were out of line and are changed: Extreme had stages that took the skilled bot 15 to 24 tries, and Endless's first mini boss ended most runs of the weaker bots. Bot runs in round 4 confirm both: the average bot now passes Endless's wave 5 in five runs of six, and on Extreme no stage took the skilled bot more than 9 tries.
- **Bugs.** Two reviews of the new code found five real bugs (one, then four), and seven more problems turned up on screenshots, in the tools and in the tests. All twelve are fixed.
- **Tests.** The full suite passes: **2511 of 2511**, up from 2159 at V1.5.0.
- **Not published.** The zip is built and smoke-run. It waits for your go.

## Choices worth knowing

Your answers on 2026-11-11: redesign all four areas; match the title and the Journey; an off-screen window for screenshots; build and wait for your go. You also asked me not to run every sub-agent on the most expensive model, so the building was done on cheaper models, with the strongest ones kept for the map art kit, the title, the bosses, Lucian and the reviews. For round 3 you picked all four of my suggestions and added the title's splash art, the skins and the Journey.

What I decided for you:

- **Resolve is a new rule.** I added it because no number change let the novice through 2-1 without making the game trivial for everyone else. It is temporary (it ends when the stage is cleared), it never applies to a cleared stage, Training or Endless, and it can be turned off. Say so if you don't want it.
- **The summon cap changes Act 5.** Before, the skilled bot needed 744 kills and 25 minutes for 5-5 and lost 5-4 seven times in a row. A player could also farm a quarter of each summon's gold and XP for ever.
- **What "novice" means.** With Resolve, how well the bot dodges barely changes its stage time, because its deaths are quick. What matters is how fast it kills. So the novice tier now wastes 25% of its fight decisions on an aimless step, never kites, backs away only below 25% health, misses half the warnings and every bolt. That is my definition; the times below depend on it.
- **"Play time" is an estimate.** The game's own story clock also runs on the end screen and the main screen. The bot adds 45 s (skilled), 75 s (average) or 120 s (novice) for each visit to the main screen and 10, 15 or 20 s for each Retry.
- **More waves, then a little more health.** More waves alone had diminishing returns: longer stages pay more gold and levels, so Lucian gets stronger. The last 10% came from enemy health per act, which adds 8% in Act 2 and 22% in Act 6, so the early game stays gentle.
- **Boss health follows the gold.** Each boss's health ÷ the gold earned before it is now between 0.67 and 1.88 (V1.5.0: 0.69 to 2.01), so no boss became a wall.
- **The tutorial has Resolve too.** A lost tutorial is still GAME OVER, and the next try gets a stack.
- **The End** hides Thresh and keeps his broken chain on the ground.

Round 3:

- **"The splash art on the main screen"** I read as the title picture, since the main screen has no splash of its own. Tell me if you meant something else.
- **Three new rules are mine:** the traits, the gift and the forge. Each is small and can be taken out alone. The forge's rate (120 blue) and the boons' sizes are first guesses that the bot runs did not contradict.
- **The novice got faster.** Before round 3 the novice bot took 8 h 20 and 8 h 37; now 6 h 43 and 7 h 34. The gift is the cause: the novice used to lose 19 tries in the second half of a stage, and now loses 10 to 12 after the pick. The skilled bot is 10 to 15 minutes slower, because traits cost it more than the gift gives. If you want the novice nearer 8 hours, the lever is the size of the boons; I left it, because you found the early game too hard before.
- **Extreme has a small Resolve now.** The difficulty is fixed once a game starts, so a stage that takes 20 tries there is a dead end. Its scale (×1.3) was your choice in V1.3 and is unchanged. With the Gameplay setting off, Extreme plays with no Resolve as before.
- **Two looks changed colour:** the Rift Scuttler is green-teal and the Elder Dragon pale violet. Both read better on their maps.
- **The three old title SVGs are deleted,** as the title paints its figures in code.

Round 4 (your "run another round", with the item icons and the in-game stat icons added):

- **No frame on an item icon.** The game already draws a rim by item kind around every cell, so the frame in the picture was a second one and cost the object a third of its size. Say so if you miss the gold frames.
- **Two passes on the item icons.** I had them drawn on a cheaper model first. About a third of that set lost clarity against the old icons (the boots all came from one template; the Recurve Bow, the Black Cleaver, the Infinity Edge and Archangel's Staff read worse than before). A stronger model then drew the 16 boots by hand and redrew 18 of the weapon and magic icons, each from what its old picture was.
- **The Evolution Tree's stat pictures are unchanged.** They are a separate, larger set. Three of their symbols now differ from the HUD's: attack speed (crossed swords), ability power (an orb over a book) and magic resist (a rune).
- **The boots' recipes sit after the legendaries.** On a backpack with nothing to craft you scroll down to the BOOTS heading; a recipe you can make jumps to the top either way. Say so if you want the boots first, or the two lists on tabs.
- **No new rule and no balance change in round 4.** The round's bot runs only checked round 3's two changes.

### Hard, Extreme and Easy

One run each, first on the numbers before round 3 and then on round 3's. The round 3 runs were stopped before they finished (see "What went wrong on the way"), so each is given as far as it got.

| Run | Before round 3 | Round 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Skilled, Hard | 4 h 36, about 4 h 55 of play; 32 tries, at most 5 (5-2) | Stopped after 6-2 at 4 h 12: 33 tries, at most 4. About 20 minutes behind the first run at that point. |
| Average, Hard | 6 h 12, about 6 h 56 of play; 56 tries, at most 11 (5-2) | Stopped in 5-5 at 4 h 17: 50 tries, at most 7 (5-2). |
| Novice, Easy | 4 h 37, about 5 h 39 of play; 52 tries, at most 6 | Stopped after 6-3 at 4 h 13: 50 tries, at most 5. Level with the first run at that point. |
| Skilled, Extreme (no Resolve) | 5-1 took 21 tries, 5-2 24, 5-5 17; stopped in Act 6 at 8 h 52 with 97 tries | 3-2 took 15 tries, 4-3 7, 5-1 5; stopped in 5-2 at 4 h 17. |

- **Hard and Easy need no change.** Hard took the skilled bot 4 h 55 of play (Normal: 4 h 13 and 4 h 40 on the same numbers), and Easy took the novice 5 h 39 (Normal: 8 h 20 and 8 h 37). No stage took more than 11 tries.
- **Extreme had walls.** With no Resolve, one bad stage cost the skilled bot 15 to 24 tries, and which stage it was moved with the luck of the run. Extreme now has Resolve at 3% a defeat (15% at most): by the sixth try the damage Lucian takes is about Hard's. A skilled run in round 4 shows the result: 3-2 took 4 tries, 5-1 4, 5-2 4, 5-4 9 and 5-5 3. It was through 6-2 after 5 h 45 and 51 tries (without Resolve: 8 h 52 and 97 tries at that point). Then the machine ran low on memory again and the run was stopped, so 6-3 to 6-5 were not played.

### Endless

Each tier played Endless from the save its Normal story run ended with (the whole story cleared, most of the tree bought), on a random map.

| Tier | Runs | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Skilled | 1 (three more were stopped) | Wave 40 when the run's 90-minute limit ended it, unbeaten: 12,624 blue and 137 orange. |
| Average | 4 | Three fell to wave 5's mini boss after 4 to 5 minutes (about 400 blue, 1 to 3 orange). One passed it and reached wave 41 in 53 minutes: 13,782 blue and 160 orange. |
| Novice | 4 | Three fell at wave 5. One reached wave 20 in 17 minutes: 4,227 blue and 56 orange. |

- **Wave 5 was a cliff.** The first mini boss comes with a full wave while Lucian is level 8 to 10 with two items. Whoever got past it lasted at least 15 more waves. It now deals 70% of its power; a mini boss from Acts 5 and 6 already dealt 80% of its own there, so 56%. Checked in round 4 with six runs a tier, each stopped at 25 waves: the average bot reached wave 25 five times and fell in wave 6 once; the novice reached wave 25 twice and wave 11 once, and fell at or before wave 5 three times (before the change: three of four).
- **What Endless pays.** The story pays about 4,500 to 4,800 blue and about 30 orange an hour of stage time. A long Endless run pays 8,400 to 15,600 blue and 90 to 200 orange an hour; a run that ends at wave 5 pays about the story's rate. After the story a skilled player lacks about 21,000 blue and 210 orange for the whole tree: two to three hours of Endless.
- **Past wave 5, Endless is long before it is hard.** A finished-story Lucian was still unbeaten at wave 40. I did not change that.
