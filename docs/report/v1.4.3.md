# V1.4.3 report: bug fixes from the V1.4.2 review

**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Version:** 1.4.3 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v1.4.3.md` · **Branch:** `v1.4.3` (from `v1.4.2`)

## Summary

V1.4.3 fixes the 19 bugs (B1–B19) from the "Lantern Review 1.4.2" artifact, plus two changes you asked for: difficulty leaves the gear menu, and Lucian's Journey token follows the selected place. There is one commit per item, in your order: B1, B3, B4, B2, B5, B6, B7, B8, B9, B10, B11, B12, B13, B14, B15, B16, B17, B18, B19, Change 1 and Change 2. B5 and B8 each have a follow-up commit right after them.

- **Combat:** Death's Dance's delayed damage can't be dodged by unequipping it. Guardian Angel's stasis stops only buff timers. Lightslinger's second shot is never lost. Cone warnings count Lucian's body. Elise doesn't Rappel while her web flies.
- **Controls:** a quick cast with indicator let go during CC fires when it ends. With Cast queue off, nothing waits.
- **The shop:** Craft inputs hold their items out of the backpack, and Craft asks first. Locked merchant slots explain themselves. The backpack recentres when the window changes.
- **The stage:** Blast Cones show their reach and where Lucian would land. Quitting during a boss cutscene keeps the clear. Debug tools leave nothing broken.
- **The rest:** live buff cards, Endless burns that scale, stale texts, a safer settings file, and a calmer main screen.

The full suite passes except the known flaky test, which passes alone: **2014 of 2015**, up from 1948. A review of the whole branch found nothing serious; its three small findings are fixed (see [Tests](#tests)).

## The fixes

| # | Item | What it was | The fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Death's Dance survives unequipping | Taking Death's Dance off for a moment (or selling it and pressing Undo sale) erased the 30% still owed. | `ItemPassives.refresh()` no longer clears the bleed. It runs out on its own, and only Defy and Guardian Angel clear it. The Ignore Pain icon stays while damage is owed, with or without the item. |
| B3 | No Rappel while Cocoon flies | Elise could start Rappel while her web was in the air. The web's stun then left Lucian no time to step out. | Elise counts her webs in flight. Rappel waits while one flies or Lucian is stunned, and keeps its cooldown. |
| B4 | Quitting during a boss cutscene | Closing the window during Act 1's or 6-5's cutscene and saving lost the clear and the boss's rewards. | `Game._save()` skips the running cutscene first (new `Stage.skip_cutscene()`), so the clear and rewards run, then saves as the result panel does. `ending_seen` isn't set, so The End still shows on the next 6-5 clear. |
| B2 | Live buff and stat cards | An open buff card froze, and could describe the old buff after a new one moved into its icon. | `BuffIcon` and `StatCell` have a `tooltip_version()` (a buff's id, count, time left to 0.1 s and text; a stat's value and breakdown), so the open card rebuilds when it changes. |
| B5 | Stasis stops only buff timers | Guardian Angel's stasis froze item cooldowns, while skill and summoner cooldowns ran. | Item cooldowns and the attack timer now count down during stasis. Only buff timers stop: speed and stat buffs, shields (Barrier's included), Ghost, potions and heals over time, and item buffs such as Spear of Shojin's Focused Will. Item waits that act like cooldowns keep running. |
| B6 | Lightslinger's second shot | A fast charged attack after E overwrote the waiting second shot. | A charged attack that comes while a second shot is waiting fires that shot at once (damage and E cut), then arms its own. |
| B7 | Short key names on cards | Fix H's doc said cards keep full names, but they showed short ones. | Your call: cards keep the short names, so a long name can't overflow a skill's box. `docs/ui_design.md` and the `short_key_label` comment say so, and a test checks both cards. |
| B8 | The Craft tab | One fragment could fill both inputs, and the note then said the recipe didn't exist. | Your design. An item in a Craft input leaves the backpack view: its cell draws empty and can't be dragged, sold or dropped on. It comes back when dragged out, on a right-click on the input, when the tab changes, or when the merchant closes. Craft asks first ("Craft X from Y and Z for N gold?"). A craft empties both inputs, so the result shows in its cell. |
| B9 | Locked merchant slots | Right-clicking a chained slot said "Already sold", and it had no hover card. | New `Shop.Result.SLOT_LOCKED` gives "That slot is locked". The hover note says "Buy Merchant Stock in the Evolution Tree". |
| B10 | Blast Cone reach and landing | (Your call: being thrown into lava is a feature.) Nothing showed how close Lucian had to be, or where he'd land. | A ripe cone draws a faint yellow ring at its 350 px reach, brighter while Lucian is inside. While a shot would throw him, a body-sized ring and a faint dashed line show where he'd land, moving as he moves. The marks sit over the lava and under the units. |
| B11 | Quick cast through CC | With quick cast with indicator, letting go of the key during CC lost the cast. | The aim stays and fires once Lucian can act, where the cursor is then. Right-click or ESC cancels it, and death drops any aim. |
| B12 | Cast queue off, nothing waits | An out-of-reach Gunblade or Smite pressed during a cast time walked into reach afterwards and replaced the attack order. | `_walk_into_reach` refuses while Lucian is cast-locked or dashing, so with the queue off the press is refused and the attack order kept. |
| B13 | Endless burns scale | Burns from normal and elite enemies stayed the same in every Endless wave. | New `EnemyUnit.burn_scale`, set by the Endless director to `damage / data.damage`. For a boss this equals its power, so it isn't scaled twice. Story burns are unchanged. |
| B14 | Cone warnings and Lucian's body | Half of Lucian could stand in a cone's red at its sides and take nothing. | `in_cone` widens the half-angle by `asin(r / d)`, so his 30 px body counts at the sides, as for circles, lines and rings. |
| B15 | Hidden screens stand still | The Journey animated while hidden, and every section kept drawing under Settings. | The Journey processes only while it shows. While Settings is open, the section area's processing is off, so the Tree, the Skin Shop and the Journey stand still until it closes. |
| B16 | The backpack recentres | After the window changed shape mid-stage, the backpack opened off-centre. | The backpack places its column again on the viewport's `size_changed`. |
| B17 | Four stale texts | The Boots node, the Potions hint, the `endless_map` doc row and the crafting note were out of date. | The Boots node points to the Craft tab. The Potions hint names the potion keys as bound now and follows rebinds. The design doc says 0 is Random and 1–6 the maps. `docs/items.md` says a legendary sells for less than its fragments plus the fee. |
| B18 | Debug tools | A wave jump mid-dash left Dark Lucian's trail. A debug-spawned `thresh_1` froze at 50% and his wave never ended. | Dark Lucian's `_exit_tree()` finishes his trail. Every plain Thresh is wired: the boss wave's plays the cutscene, and any other leaves at 50% with his blue and orange, and the fight goes on. |
| B19 | A safer settings file | A failed rename could delete the only good copy, and each slider step wrote the file. | A failed rename keeps `settings.json.tmp`, and loading falls back to it when `settings.json` is missing or broken. A slider applies live while dragged and saves once when let go. |
| Change 1 | No difficulty in the gear menu | The gear menu showed a greyed Difficulty row. | The row is gone. Difficulty is chosen only on the title's difficulty page after "New game". |
| Change 2 | The Journey token follows the pick | The token sat over the next open stage. | The token floats over the selected place (a stage, Training or the Endless portal) and moves with the pick. The Journey still opens on the next open stage, so the token starts there. |

## Choices worth knowing

Your decisions:

- **B5:** in stasis, skill, summoner and item cooldowns count; only buff timers stop.
- **B7:** cards keep the short key names.
- **B8:** the input holds the item out of the backpack, and it comes back on drag-out, a tab change or leaving the shop. Craft asks first.
- **B10:** lava landings are a feature; the cone shows its reach and the landing spot instead.
- **B15 and B16:** as you described.
- **Changes 1 and 2:** as you described.

Where the build had to choose:

- **B5:**
  - Item waits that act like cooldowns keep running in stasis: Kaenic Rookern's Magebane wait, Malignance's Hatefog cooldown, Rapid Firecannon's wait, and the combat timer behind Jak'Sho. That last one means Jak'Sho can still gain a stack during a stasis.
  - HP and mana regen stay stopped, as before (Lucian's HP is held at 0 until the revive).
- **B8:**
  - The inventory model is unchanged: the item stays in its cell, so a save or crash loses nothing, and the cell counts as taken.
  - A fee you can't afford is refused at once with "Not enough gold", without asking.
  - Dropping an input's item onto a full input swaps the two. Only a free cell takes an item dragged out of an input; anywhere else, it goes back to its own cell.
- **B11:** the waiting aim follows the cursor and fires where the cursor is when the CC ends.
- **B13:** story enemies' hits already grow with the act and wave, so scaling their burns by the same ratio would have made Act 3's story burns hotter. To keep the story unchanged, the ratio applies only in Endless. Scaling story burns too would be a one-line change.
- **B15:** Settings' backdrop is see-through, so the sections aren't hidden under it, only stopped. The main backdrop's motes keep moving, since they weren't on your list.
- **B18:** `thresh_1` stays in the debug list and is wired to leave at 50%, rather than being dropped.
- **Change 2:** with every stage cleared, the Journey opens on Training and the token sits there. Before, the token disappeared.
