# V1.3.1 report: nine bug fixes from the code review

**Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Version:** 1.3.1 · **Source:** the V1.3.0 code review (bugs 2–10)

## Summary

The V1.3.0 code review found 10 bugs. This release fixes **bugs 2 to 10**, one commit each, in the order you asked for: 3, 4, 5, 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. Each fix follows the review's fix idea, except two, which follow your calls:

- **Bug 7:** a click on the tree's buy bar asks first, with a table. The review's idea was to ignore the second click of a double-click.
- **Bug 9:** Refresh is refused, and its button greyed out, once no unsold slot is left.

Bug 1 (crafting then selling makes gold) and the smaller findings (11 and up) are not in this release.

## The fixes

| # | Bug | What caused it | The fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Skills fire after Lucian dies | Q's beam, W's missile, the passive's second shot and Galeforce's missiles wait on timers in their own nodes, and nothing cancelled them. A stun or a death mid-Galeforce dash even fired the missiles at once. | Each one gets a `cancel()`. `Lucian._drop_in_flight`, which death, stasis, stuns, fears, displacements and throws already call, now calls them all. |
| 4 | Guardian Angel freezes burns and CC | Stasis returned before burns, slows and CC ticked and kept them, so he revived still stunned or burning. New burns and slows landed during it. | Stasis clears burns, slows and CC, as death does. `add_slow` and `add_burn` refuse new ones during stasis. |
| 5 | Boss warnings strike after the boss dies | A ground warning never checked its caster: the Infernal Drake's meteors and Fire Breath, the revived Karthus's Lay Waste, and Elder Breath. | A warning takes its hit's attacker as its caster. Once the caster is dead or gone, the warning goes at once, with no hit, as Elder's Mark already did. |
| 2 | Every stage start resets the window | A stage called the full settings apply, and that un-maximized, resized and centred the window. | New `GameSettings.apply_engine()`: audio, input and display, without the window. A stage under the Game uses it. A stage run alone (F6, tests, screenshots) still does the full apply. |
| 6 | Smite and Gunblade hit the wrong enemy | They picked the in-reach enemy nearest the cursor, so a minion in reach won over a boss under the cursor. | The enemy under the cursor comes first. Out of reach, Lucian walks to it through the existing walk-then-cast. With none under the cursor, the old rule stays. |
| 7 | A double-click buys two tree levels | Every click on the cost bar bought a level. | A click opens **BUY UPGRADE** (`TreePurchasePopup`, see below). Buy buys one level, once. |
| 8 | Loot on Act 6 ledges is lost | Loot dropped where the enemy died, and a perched warden's ledge is 260 out over the abyss. | A drop that lands off the ground moves toward the merchant until it is 20 inside walkable ground (`StageMap.walkable_toward`, which `Boss.landing_spot` now uses too). |
| 9 | Refresh charges gold for nothing | Refresh charged first, then re-rolled only the unsold slots. | `MerchantStock.has_unsold()` counts the open slots only. Refresh is refused for free with "Nothing left to refresh", and the button greys out. |
| 10 | Akshan's Heroic Swing can end in a wall | His position was set straight onto the swing's circle. | Each step of the swing goes through `Boss.landing_spot`, as the Elder Dragon, Elise and Maokai do: he slides along a wall's edge. |

### The buy question (bug 7)

It uses the menus' ornate card, like the skin purchase question.

| Row | Shows |
|---|---|
| Title | **BUY UPGRADE**, the node's name under it |
| Level | "LVL 2 → 3 / 5" |
| Effect | the value now → after |
| Price | the blue and orange it costs, or **Free** |
| You have | blue and orange owned (hidden when free) |
| After purchase | what is left, red when short (hidden when free) |

- A note reads "Only Reset tree gives essence back."
- **Buy** is off while the level can't be bought. **Cancel**, ESC and leaving the tree close it; ESC keeps the pick.
- **The double-click:** the first click opens the question. Its backdrop takes the second click, and its buttons sit clear of the cost bar (a test checks the rects).
- **Free levels** (E after Act 1) ask too, for one rule everywhere.

### Choices worth knowing

- **Shots already flying still land** (bug 3): a W missile in the air, R's bullets. Only a shot still waiting to fire is dropped. Mana and cooldowns stay spent, as in LoL.
- **A siege minion's shell goes with its minion** (bug 5). It is a warning with a caster like the bosses'. A specter's blast already did this.
- **Short gold** keeps Refresh lit (bug 9): it is refused with "Not enough gold", as before. Only "nothing to re-roll" greys it out.
