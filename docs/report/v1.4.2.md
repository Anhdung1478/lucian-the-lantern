# V1.4.2 report: improvements and minor fixes from the code review

**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Version:** 1.4.2 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/v1.4.2.md` · **Branch:** `v1.4.2` (from `v1.4.1`)

## Summary

V1.4.2 builds the review's improvements and minor extras you picked, one commit each, in the order you asked for: 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 32 to 44, then A, B, C, D, E, G, H, I, J, K and L.

- **Orders and casts:** Lucian keeps his last click through crowd control, and item actives and summoner spells queue like skills.
- **Menus and the shop:** arrow keys and Enter work the menus, the shop can undo sales, shop cards show recipe hints, and empty equip slots take items before "No room".
- **Saving:** Start saves first, difficulty is chosen only at a new game, the Endless map is remembered, and a failed new-game save no longer leaves a half-made game.
- **The rest** is smaller: boss and burn tweaks, a few redraw savings, short key names, and a handful of small bugs.

Each item was reviewed, and a final review of the whole branch found no correctness or save-safety defects. Its ten small findings went into a fix wave.

The full suite passes: **1948 tests**, up from 1828 (see [Tests](#tests)).

## Choices worth knowing

Your answers:

- **42 (Honeyfruit):** it restores HP and mana together, and isn't eaten when both are full.
- **28 (save on Start):** a failed save blocks the start, the same as G.
- **26:** difficulty is fixed when a new game starts. Old saves keep the level they have.
- **29:** like LoL, the last move or attack order survives a CC and runs when it ends, whether it was a click given during the CC or the order Lucian had before it.
- **30:** item actives and summoner spells queue like skills, in one shared slot where the latest press wins. R is a channel, not a lock, so nothing waits through it. Potions stay instant.
- **33:** arrow keys and Enter, not Space (which is Center camera), in the title menu and its pages, the stage pause menu, the gear menu and its slot page, and every confirm pop-up. Enter confirms.
- **C:** of overlapping burns with the same id, the strongest wins.

The controller's rulings:

- **Ruling 1:** item 26 may delete `test_set_difficulty_changes_it_in_memory_only`, because `SaveGame.set_difficulty` is gone. "Rewritten, not deleted" protects behaviour that still exists.
- **Ruling 2:** implementers ran only the test files they touched. The controller ran the full suite in a separate worktree alongside each batch review.
- **Ruling 3:** items were built in batches of consecutive items, with one commit per item and one review per batch.
- **Ruling 4:** screenshots were skipped (see [Not done](#not-done)).
- **Ruling 5:** with the cast queue off, a summoner spell pressed during Q's or W's cast time is now refused. It used to fire. You asked for "exactly like skills", and skills are refused then too.
- **Ruling 6:** `MenuKeys` lights nothing until the first arrow key, and the mouse moves the light only once something is lit. Mouse-only play looks as it did. Confirm pop-ups start with their first button lit, so there the mouse always moves the light.
- **Ruling 7:** the bots ran at `31c02e6`, before the final fix wave, to save time. The Bots section names the commit.

What the implementers chose:

- **Item 26:** the greyed row's hint shows the level name. The row's menu entry no longer connects an empty callable.
- **Item 27:** the scaled orange uses rounding, so 5 × 1.3 is 7. Extra blue and extra orange aren't scaled.
- **Item 29:**
  - The A key and left and right clicks work during CC. Skill, item, summoner and potion keys and Ctrl rank-ups don't.
  - The `move_dropped` signal is no longer emitted by a stun, fear, displacement or launch (a dash or blink still emits it), so a held right-click keeps steering after the CC.
  - A normal-cast aim waits out the CC instead of firing a refused cast.
  - A merchant clicked during CC opens once it ends, not at once. A review found this and it was fixed in the batch's fix round.
- **Item 30:** the queue gate sits in `use_equip_slot` and `use_summoner`. A rooted Flash or item dash is refused, not queued. The HUD still shows only a queued skill.
- **Item 32:** the Journey reads the saved map on every refresh, so debug "Reset progress" sends the chip back to Random.
- **Item 33:**
  - A held Enter (an echo) is ignored, so it can't answer the next pop-up. Arrows do repeat.
  - Pop-ups open with their first button lit, so Enter confirms at once, even on "Overwrite slot" and "Reset tree".
  - Not covered: the skin and tree purchase pop-ups, the stage result panel, The End and Settings.
- **Item 35:** the wall test starts after the wave has flown `hit_radius` (90), so a wave fired beside a wall doesn't vanish at the boss's feet.
- **Item 38:** `Shop.Result.CELL_TAKEN` and its panel message became unreachable and were removed.
- **Item 40:** "Upgrades your Boots into X" comes after the "Builds into" lines and isn't counted in the 3-line cap. A fragment that pairs with itself (Tear + Tear) counts as held when you own one.
- **Item 41:**
  - The Undo sale button shows on every tab, since you can sell from any tab.
  - A new result, `NOTHING_TO_UNDO`, is kept as a safety.
  - Limit: the stacks and cooldowns of an equipped item that is sold and bought back aren't restored.
- **Item 43:** a review found the new cadence test could fail under CPU load (see the final fix wave in [Tests](#tests)).
- **Item H:** Godot names the key "Backspace", not "BackSpace", so the short map and the test use that. Mouse 4 and 5 are "M4" and "M5". The final review also had the brackets, backslash, slash and keypad operators given their own short labels, so they don't all collapse to the same five letters.
- **Item I:** a sound is counted free or busy by its own start and end time, not by `playing`, so the choice is the same headless.
- **Item J:** only `EnemyUnit`s under the world go. The training dummy and Senna are left alone.

**What this says:**

- **Both bots finish the story, with no script errors.** No stage walled under the 25-try cap.
- **The changes cancel out, within the bots' usual spread.**
  - The skilled bot is an hour slower. Most of that is one 40-minute failed try at 6-4, three extra tries at 3-5, and an extra try at 3-2.
  - The average bot is 1 h 43 faster. Most of that is 6-5, which took 1 try against 9.
  - As before, read the times as ±30–60 minutes. The same numbers can give a stage 1 try or 10.
- **The items that can move the numbers** all help Lucian a little:
  - **39:** empty equip slots now take items.
  - **42:** Honeyfruit restores mana too and waits while both bars are full.
  - **35:** wide waves stop on walls.
  - **36:** no Rappel during a stun.
  
  C (the strongest burn wins) can make a mixed burn a little stronger in 3-2.
- **The Elder Dragon's 3-5 is still the average bot's wall:** 20 tries against 19. V1.4.1's three options to soften it still apply: more kill gold in Acts 3–6, a lower craft fee, or a cheaper 3-5. The bot can measure any of them.
- **The skilled bot is now at 5 h 41,** inside the 5–7 h target for a skilled player.
