# Vietnamese glossary: Lucian, The Lantern

One Vietnamese term per English term. Confidence: official (exact name seen on a source), likely (common among players, client wording unconfirmed), own (no official name found; natural translation).

Source key:
- ddragon = ddragon.leagueoflegends.com, vi_VN data files of patch 16.22.1 (item.json, summoner.json, champion/*.json). This is the data the Vietnamese client uses.
- lor = dd.b.pvp.net (Legends of Runeterra, vi_vn globals)
- wr = wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/vi-vn
- support = support.riotgames.com/vi-vn
- fandom = lienminh.fandom.com (Vietnamese LoL wiki)
- web = other Vietnamese pages (vnesports.net, thanhnien.vn, gam.gg)

Rules: champion names stay as they are (Lucian, Senna, Thresh, Akshan, Maokai, Elise, Karthus, Hecarim, Viego). Capitalisation follows the client (every word capitalised in names). Keywords inside sentences (choáng, làm chậm) are lower case.
